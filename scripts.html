分场景双语话术库Phrase Book by Scenario

覆盖船上最常见的工作场景，上岗第一天就能用的 36 条真实话术。方括号内容为可替换变量，按实际情况套用即可。

全部门适用 36 条场景话术 中英对照

日常问候与指引Greetings & Directions

晨间问候：在走廊或电梯遇到宾客/同事时的标准早安问候。
Good morning! Beautiful day today, isn't it? How can I help you?
指引方向：告知宾客目的地所在甲板与行走路线。
Go straight ahead and turn left at the atrium — it's on Deck 5, just past the shops.
电梯/楼梯指引：宾客询问电梯或楼梯位置时使用。
The elevators are just around the corner to your right. If you'd like some exercise, the stairs are right next to them.
电梯间闲聊安全回应：宾客在电梯里闲聊时的礼貌回应，顺势提供服务。
Yes, it's a lovely ship, isn't it? If there's anything you need, just dial Guest Services from your stateroom — we're available around the clock.
送别：宾客离开餐厅/区域时的礼貌送别用语。
Thank you for stopping by — enjoy the rest of your evening, and I hope to see you again soon!
打扰致歉：需要在宾客交谈或用餐时插入服务时的开场。
Excuse me for interrupting — may I just take a moment to top up your water?

客房部场景Housekeeping

敲门进入：清扫客房前的标准敲门与自我通报话术。
Housekeeping! May I come in?
询问清洁时间偏好：尊重宾客作息，确认打扫时段。
Good afternoon! Would you prefer I service your stateroom now, or would a later time be more convenient?
毛巾艺术留言：完成特殊布置（毛巾艺术、装饰）后留下的温馨便条。
A little surprise from your room steward — we hope it makes you smile. Enjoy your cruise!
报修跟进：向宾客通报维修已完成并确认是否正常。
Your maintenance request has been completed. Could you please check if everything is working properly now? If not, I'll send engineering back right away.
道别祝愿：打扫完毕离开客房时的礼貌收尾。
All done! Is there anything else I can get for you? Have a wonderful day!
勿扰牌回应：看到「请勿打扰」标识时的处理与留言方式。
I see the Do Not Disturb sign — I'll come back later. I'll slip a card under the door so you can let us know a convenient time.

餐饮部场景Food & Beverage

迎宾引座：宾客到达餐厅时的迎宾与入座引导。
Good evening, welcome to the [restaurant name]! Do you have a reservation? A table for two by the window is ready for you — right this way, please.
点单确认：确认宾客是否准备好点餐，避免催促感。
Are you ready to order, or would you like a few more minutes to look over the menu?
推荐今日特选：主动介绍当日特色菜品。
May I recommend today's special? It's a pan-seared sea bass with lemon butter sauce — one of our most popular dishes tonight.
过敏源确认：点单时必须确认饮食限制与过敏情况。
Do you have any food allergies or dietary restrictions I should let the chef know about? We're happy to accommodate any special requests.
上菜服务：呈上菜品并确认需求的服务用语。
Here is your [dish name] — please be careful, the plate is hot. Enjoy your meal! I'll check back with you shortly.
结账致谢：宾客用餐结束结账时的礼貌收尾。
It's been a pleasure serving you tonight. You can sign this to your onboard account — thank you, and we hope to see you again tomorrow evening!

前台宾客服务场景Guest Services

办理入住登记：登船日在前台为宾客办理入住的标准流程用语。
Welcome aboard! May I see your boarding pass and passport, please? Your stateroom is 8056 on Deck 8, and your cruise card also works as your ID ashore — please keep it with you at all times.
解释账单项目：宾客对账单有疑问时的解释话术。
This charge is for the specialty dining on the [date] — I can pull up the signed receipt for you right now if you'd like to double-check it.
押金说明：向宾客说明船载账户押金与结算方式。
To open your onboard account, we'll place a temporary authorization of [amount] on your credit card. Any unused amount is released automatically after the voyage — nothing is actually charged until you spend it.
投诉初步响应：按 LEARN 模型（Listen–Empathize–Apologize–Resolve–Notify）完成第一步的话术。
Thank you for bringing this to my attention. I completely understand your frustration — please tell me exactly what happened, and I'll do everything I can to make it right for you.
寄存物品：受理宾客行李或贵重物品寄存时的确认话术。
Certainly, we can keep that for you. Here's your claim tag with a number on it — please keep this safe and present it when you collect your [item] at Guest Services.
离船送别：航程结束宾客离船时的送别用语。
Thank you for sailing with us! We hope you had a wonderful cruise — have a safe trip home, and we look forward to welcoming you back on board soon!

客诉与应急场景Complaints & Emergencies

倾听安抚：宾客情绪激动时先共情安抚，不急于辩解。
I completely understand — I would feel the same way in your position. Let me note down all the details, and I promise we'll sort this out for you.
升级上报：超出自身权限时上报主管的标准话术。
I want to make sure this is handled properly, so let me get my supervisor for you right away. They'll be with you in just a moment — may I offer you a seat while you wait?
延误通知：因天气或靠港延误向宾客通报歉意的通用话术。
We apologize for the delay — due to weather conditions, our arrival into [port name] has been pushed back to [time]. Your shore excursion schedule will be adjusted accordingly, and updates will be announced on board.
应急广播标准格式：船员须熟记的公共广播开场格式（示例）。
Attention all passengers, attention all passengers. This is your captain speaking. We are conducting a routine safety drill — please proceed calmly to your muster stations. This is not an emergency.
集合站引导：紧急或演练时引导宾客前往集合站。
Please proceed to your muster station, Station [letter/number], located [location]. Take your lifejacket if it's nearby, follow the crew's directions, and walk — do not run — keeping to the right side of the corridor.
冷静疏散用语：疏散引导时保持秩序、安抚宾客的用语。
Please stay calm and follow me. Your lifejackets are available at the muster station, and my colleagues are stationed along the route to assist you. Everything is under control.

船员内部沟通Crew Communication

交接班：与上一班同事交接时确认未决事项。
You're relieved — thanks for covering. Anything I should know? Any open complaints or follow-ups from your shift?
呼叫支援：高峰期请同事临时顶岗。
Could you cover my station for five minutes? I need to check on a guest at the tender pier — I'll be right back.
向主管汇报：简洁清晰地汇报异常情况。
Excuse me, [supervisor's name], may I have a quick word? A guest in stateroom [number] reported a problem with [issue] — I've logged it, but I think you should know right away.
领班布置任务确认：接受任务时的标准确认回复。
Understood, on it. Table 12 needs priority service first — I'll take care of it right away.
请求重复：嘈杂环境下没听清指令时的礼貌确认。
Sorry, it's quite loud here — could you say that again, please? Just want to make sure I've got it right.
感谢与团队精神：同事帮忙后的真诚致谢。
Thanks so much for your help with the setup — that really saved me. Great teamwork! Let me know anytime you need a hand too.
⚠️ 通用参考声明：以上话术为行业通用版本，仅作学习参考。各船司均有品牌化的服务标准与表达风格（如 Carnival 的 Fun Squad 活泼风格、Disney 的主题化问候语），请以入职后的船上培训材料与部门手册为准。For general reference only. Each cruise line has its own branded service style — always follow your on-board training materials and department handbook.
