🛏️客房部Housekeeping Department用工量最大零经验友好▼
部门简介

客房部是全船规模最大的酒店部门，负责全部客舱、走廊与公共区域的清洁保养、布草洗涤和宾客日常服务，人数通常占酒店部员工的三分之一以上（大型邮轮约 200–400 人）。晋升通道清晰：客房管家 → 资深管家 → 客房服务主管 → 助理经理，英语门槛相对较低、招聘名额最多，是零经验新人上船最常见的切入点。

Overview

Housekeeping is the largest hotel department on board, responsible for cleaning and maintaining all staterooms, corridors and public areas, laundering linen and delivering daily guest service. It typically accounts for over a third of hotel staff (roughly 200–400 crew on large ships). With a clear promotion path — Stateroom Attendant → Senior Attendant → Floor Supervisor → Assistant Manager — moderate English requirements and the highest number of openings, it is the most common entry point for first-time crew.

细分岗位与工作内容 Sub-Positions & Detailed Duties

客房服务主管Floor Supervisor

细分工作内容

直属上级：客房部经理（Housekeeping Manager）。编制：每 30–40 间客舱设 1 名主管，每船约 8–15 名。工作内容：

  • 每日早会分派客房管家责任区，核查清洁计划与当日 VIP/退房清单
  • 逐间抽查 10–15 间客房清洁质量，按 100 分制检查表打分并现场辅导返工
  • 统计每日客房状态（OC/VC/DND 等）并与前台对账，处理加床、维修等特殊请求
  • 带教新人管家并完成在岗培训记录，跟进员工考勤与排班
  • 汇总宾客投诉与失物报告，向上级提交日报
Duties

Reports to the Housekeeping Manager. Ratio: one supervisor per 30–40 staterooms, about 8–15 per ship. Duties:

  • Assign stateroom attendants' sections at the morning briefing and review cleaning schedules plus VIP and checkout lists
  • Inspect 10–15 staterooms daily against a 100-point checklist and coach attendants on rework
  • Reconcile stateroom status (OC/VC/DND) with the Front Desk and handle special requests such as extra beds and maintenance
  • Mentor new attendants and keep on-the-job training records; manage attendance and rostering
  • Compile guest complaints and lost-and-found reports, submitting a daily summary to management.
入门要求

通常需 1–2 个合同期客房经验或岸上酒店客房主管经验，能用英语进行检查沟通与写报告。

Entry Requirements

Usually one or two contracts of housekeeping experience, or shore-side hotel floor supervisor experience; able to conduct inspections and write reports in English.

客房管家Stateroom Attendant

细分工作内容

直属上级：客房服务主管（Floor Supervisor）。编制：每管家负责 12–16 间客舱，全船约 60–120 名，为客房部核心岗位。工作内容：

  • 每日按标准清洁 12–16 间客房，单间 20–30 分钟，含铺床、除尘、吸尘与备品归位
  • 每天更换毛巾与床单（退客房全换，续住客按环保计划更换），补充洗漱用品至配额
  • 对卫浴进行消毒，检查空调、灯具、龙头并即时报修
  • 执行夜间开床服务，制作毛巾艺术并放置次日日程卡
  • 记录宾客偏好（枕头类型、加床等）并回应特殊请求
Duties

Reports to the Floor Supervisor. Ratio: each attendant covers 12–16 staterooms; 60–120 per ship, the backbone of the department. Duties:

  • Clean 12–16 staterooms daily to standard in 20–30 minutes each, including bed making, dusting, vacuuming and resetting amenities
  • Replace towels and linen every day (full change on departures, reuse program for stayovers) and replenish toiletries to par levels
  • Sanitize bathrooms, check air conditioning, lighting and faucets, and report faults promptly
  • Perform evening turndown service, fold towel animals and place the next day's program card
  • Log guest preferences (pillow type, extra bed, etc.) and respond to special requests.
入门要求

零经验可入，能进行基础英语日常沟通与服务对话即可，是全船招聘量最大的岗位。

Entry Requirements

Open to beginners; basic conversational English for guest interaction is sufficient — the position with the largest number of openings on board.

公共区域服务员Public Area Attendant

细分工作内容

直属上级：客房部经理 / 公共区域主管（Housekeeping Manager / Public Area Supervisor）。编制：每船约 15–30 名，按甲板或区域分包干。工作内容：

  • 每日清洁大堂、电梯、走廊、观景甲板等公共区域，含吸尘、除渍、玻璃与扶手擦拭
  • 操作洗地机、抛光机等设备对地毯和大理石地面进行周期性保养（每周 1–2 次）
  • 清理垃圾桶与送餐遗留托盘，维护洗手间布草与消耗品配额
  • 依夜航计划对泳池甲板、日光浴区进行高压冲洗与躺椅归位
  • 发现地毯污渍、照明故障等立即报修并跟进闭环
Duties

Reports to the Housekeeping Manager / Public Area Supervisor. Ratio: 15–30 per ship, assigned by deck or zone. Duties:

  • Clean public areas daily — lobby, elevators, corridors and open decks — including vacuuming, stain removal, glass and railing polishing
  • Operate floor scrubbers and polishers for weekly carpet and marble maintenance (1–2 times per week)
  • Empty trash bins, clear room service trays, and keep restrooms stocked with linen and consumables
  • Pressure-wash pool decks and sun areas overnight and re-stack loungers per the nightly schedule
  • Report carpet stains, lighting faults and other issues to Engineering and follow up to closure.
入门要求

零经验可入，英语要求最低（主要与同事协作），适合体力好、不怕夜班的新人。

Entry Requirements

Open to beginners with the lowest English requirement on board (mostly teamwork with colleagues); suits physically fit newcomers comfortable with night shifts.

洗衣房服务员Laundry Attendant

细分工作内容

直属上级：洗衣房经理（Laundry Master / Laundry Manager）。编制：每船约 10–25 名，分洗、烘、烫、折叠四个工位轮岗。工作内容：

  • 分拣宾客与船员衣物，按面料与颜色分类洗涤，每台工业洗衣机单次装载 20–50 公斤
  • 操作工业烘干机、熨烫机与压平机，处理床单、桌布等大件布草，日处理量可达 3–5 吨
  • 按配额清点并向客房与餐饮部门发放干净布草，登记出入库数量
  • 检查洗涤后布草质量，剔除破损、污渍件并单独返洗
  • 为宾客提供收费洗熨服务，逐件登记、打包并按时送回客舱
Duties

Reports to the Laundry Master / Laundry Manager. Ratio: 10–25 per ship, rotating through washing, drying, pressing and folding stations. Duties:

  • Sort guest and crew laundry by fabric and color; industrial washers take 20–50 kg per load
  • Operate industrial dryers, flatwork ironers and presses for sheets and table linen, processing 3–5 tons daily
  • Issue clean linen to Housekeeping and F&B against par counts, recording inventory in and out
  • Inspect laundered items, pulling stained or damaged pieces aside for re-washing
  • Provide paid guest laundry and pressing service, logging items individually and returning them to staterooms on time.
入门要求

零经验可入，英语要求低（以船员内部协作为主），高温环境作业、需耐体力。

Entry Requirements

Open to beginners with minimal English (mainly internal crew coordination); involves hot working conditions and physical stamina.

仓库管理员Storekeeper

细分工作内容

负责全船食品、酒水、客用品及消耗品的收货验货，核对供应商交货单与订货单的数量、规格及保质期，不合格品当场拒收并记录；按先进先出（FIFO）原则执行领料发放，每日为厨房、酒吧及客务部门出具领料单并核对签字；每周盘点重点库位、每月全面盘点，库存差异控制在 1% 以内，及时提交补货申请避免断货；维护库房温湿度标准（干货仓 18–22℃、冷藏库 0–4℃、冷冻库 −18℃ 以下），执行堆码与标签规范，确保通道畅通、离墙离地；登记报损与过期品报废，配合船医及卫生检查（USPH/Vessel Sanitation）通过库房环节审核。

Duties

Receive and inspect all food, beverage, guest supplies and consumables, checking delivery notes against purchase orders for quantity, specification and shelf life, and rejecting and logging substandard goods on the spot; issue requisitions to the galley, bar and housekeeping daily on a first-in, first-out (FIFO) basis with signed handovers; conduct weekly counts of key locations and a full monthly inventory, keeping variances within 1% and submitting replenishment requests in time to avoid stockouts; maintain store temperature and humidity standards (dry stores 18–22°C, chill room 0–4°C, freezers below −18°C) and stacking/labeling rules with clear aisles, goods off the floor and away from walls; record breakages and expired-item write-offs and support the galley and stores sections of USPH / Vessel Sanitation inspections.

制服管理员Uniform Room Attendant / Linen Keeper

细分工作内容

与洗衣房服务员同属客房部洗衣与布草体系，负责船员制服的发放、回收与登记，建立按部门、职级的尺寸台账；处理制服修改与缝补，协调裁缝及干洗安排，保证船员换装高峰期（登船日、换班周）48 小时内完成调换；管理制服间库存与洗涤计划，与洗衣房排定各部门制服洗涤轮次，跟踪破损率并提交订购建议；为新上船船员按职级配齐全套制服并办理签领手续，协助离船船员办理退还清点；对磨损、褪色超过标准的旧制服进行报损，登记报废数量并控制年度制服成本。

Duties

Part of the housekeeping laundry and linen operation alongside the Laundry Attendant, handling crew uniform issue, turn-in and registration with size records kept by department and rank; arrange alterations and repairs with the tailoring and dry-cleaning service so exchanges are completed within 48 hours during peak periods such as embarkation and crew changeover days; manage uniform room stock and the washing schedule, planning laundry rotation with the laundry and tracking wear rates to submit reordering recommendations; outfit newly joined crew with a full uniform set according to rank and process signed issue slips, and check returned uniforms for signing-off crew; write off worn or faded uniforms beyond standard, log disposal quantities and help keep annual uniform costs under control.

🛎️前台宾客服务部Guest Services Division英语要求高晋升快服务窗口▼
部门简介

前台宾客服务部是邮轮的「服务大脑」，设于客舱层中庭，负责登离船手续、账务结算、问询与投诉处理、岸上观光协调及 VIP 宾客服务，是全船信息流与服务流的中枢。大型邮轮约 20–40 人，多为高颜值、高英语水平的服务型人才；晋升速度快（接待 → 主管 → 经理 → 酒店副总监），英语要求全酒店部最高，是服务专业毕业生的热门目标部门。

Overview

Guest Services is the "service brain" of the ship, based in the atrium, handling embarkation and disembarkation, billing and folios, inquiries and complaints, shore excursion coordination and VIP services — the hub of onboard information and service flow. Large ships employ 20–40 staff, mostly polished, fluent English speakers; promotion is fast (Receptionist → Supervisor → Manager → Hotel Director track) and the English bar is the highest in Hotel Operations, making it a top target for hospitality graduates.

细分岗位与工作内容 Sub-Positions & Detailed Duties

宾客服务经理Guest Services Manager

细分工作内容

汇报关系：向酒店总监（Hotel Director / F&B & Hotel Operations Director）汇报，管理前台 20–40 名员工。工作内容：

  • 统筹前台 24 小时排班与登离船日高峰人力调配（单日吞吐 2,000–6,000 名宾客）
  • 处理升级投诉与重大服务事故，24 小时内闭环并出具宾客补偿方案
  • 每日与岸上各部门对账：审核应收账、退款与烟酒限额报表
  • 主持宾客满意度分析（问卷与评论评分），向酒店总监提交改进计划
  • 负责船司会员计划（如 Crown & Anchor、Latitudes）权益执行与贵宾接待
Duties

Reports to the Hotel Director, managing 20–40 Guest Services staff. Duties:

  • Oversee 24-hour front desk rostering and peak-day staffing for embarkation/debarkation (2,000–6,000 guests per turnaround)
  • Handle escalated complaints and major service incidents, closing them within 24 hours with compensation proposals
  • Reconcile daily accounts with shore-side offices: receivables, refunds and bar/tobacco allowance reports
  • Lead guest satisfaction analysis (surveys and review scores) and present action plans to the Hotel Director
  • Administer loyalty program benefits (e.g. Crown & Anchor, Latitudes) and host VIP guests.
英语要求

英语流利（CEFR C1 水平），需处理投诉谈判、撰写英文报告，通常要求 2–3 个合同期前台经验。

English Requirement

Fluent English (CEFR C1) for complaint negotiation and report writing; usually requires 2–3 contracts of front desk experience.

前台接待Receptionist

细分工作内容

汇报关系：向宾客服务经理（Guest Services Manager）或前台主管汇报，每班 3–8 人轮班（24 小时三班倒）。工作内容：

  • 办理登船 Check-in 与离船结账，高峰日每人日均服务 150–300 名宾客
  • 解答问询并处理房务、餐饮类投诉，使用「LAST」话术（Listen–Apologize–Solve–Thank）
  • 管理宾客船卡（Sail & Sign 卡等）挂失补办、押金与每日账单核对
  • 处理外汇兑换、邮资、船岸通讯收费等杂项业务
  • 登记失物招领、传递宾客留言与医疗求助转介
Duties

Reports to the Guest Services Manager or Front Desk Supervisor; 3–8 staff per shift on a 24-hour rotation. Duties:

  • Handle check-in and checkout; each agent serves 150–300 guests on turnaround days
  • Answer inquiries and resolve housekeeping and F&B complaints using the LAST technique (Listen–Apologize–Solve–Thank)
  • Manage guest cruise cards (e.g. Sail & Sign): lost card reissue, deposits and daily folio checks
  • Process currency exchange, postage and ship-to-shore communication charges
  • Log lost and found items, relay guest messages and refer medical requests.
英语要求

英语口语流利（相当于雅思 5.5–6.5），能独立完成英语接待、投诉安抚与账单解释。

English Requirement

Fluent spoken English (IELTS 5.5–6.5 equivalent) to independently handle reception, complaint resolution and billing explanations.

游轮协调员Cruise Coordinator

细分工作内容

汇报关系：向宾客服务经理 / 岸上观光经理（Cruise Staff Manager 或 Shore Excursions Manager）汇报，每船 2–6 名。工作内容：

  • 协调岸上观光：核对每日下船团名单、分组登车（每团 45–50 人）并发布集合时间
  • 携带扩音器在舷口与码头引导宾客上下船，清点人数、防范漏乘
  • 解答口岸签证、货币、交通等问询，处理行程变更与退款申请（转至岸上客服）
  • 配合港口海关与移民局完成登离船名单申报
  • 兼任活动现场主持助理，协助宾客观光问答台运营
Duties

Reports to the Guest Services Manager / Shore Excursions Manager; 2–6 per ship. Duties:

  • Coordinate shore excursions: verify daily tour manifests, organize coach boarding (45–50 guests per group) and publish meeting times
  • Guide guests on gangways and piers with a megaphone, counting heads to prevent late returns
  • Answer questions on port visas, currency and transport; process itinerary changes and refund requests (routed to shore-side customer service)
  • Liaise with port customs and immigration on passenger manifests for embarkation/debarkation
  • Assist at event hosting and operate the shore excursion Q&A desk.
英语要求

英语口语流利且需公众表达能力，能面对数百名宾客用英语清晰广播与讲解。

English Requirement

Fluent spoken English with public-speaking ability, able to address hundreds of guests clearly over PA announcements.

礼宾部Concierge

细分工作内容

汇报关系：向宾客服务经理汇报，每船 2–8 名，套房与会员宾客专属服务岗。工作内容：

  • 为套房宾客（约占总宾客 5–15%）提供专属礼宾服务，含预订餐厅、Spa 与专享活动
  • 协调特殊庆祝安排：生日、求婚、纪念日布置与蛋糕赠送（每日 3–10 单）
  • 预订定制岸上观光与私家导游，跟进岸上游全程反馈
  • 管理贵宾酒廊（Concierge Lounge）茶点补充与开放秩序
  • 收集 VIP 宾客偏好并录入宾客档案（CRM），确保下次航程延续服务
Duties

Reports to the Guest Services Manager; 2–8 per ship, dedicated to suite guests and loyalty members. Duties:

  • Provide butler-style concierge service to suite guests (about 5–15% of total guests), booking specialty dining, spa and exclusive events
  • Coordinate special occasions: birthdays, proposals and anniversary setups with cake deliveries (3–10 orders daily)
  • Arrange bespoke shore excursions and private guides, following up on feedback throughout
  • Maintain the Concierge Lounge: replenishing refreshments and managing access
  • Log VIP guest preferences in the CRM guest profile so service carries over to the next cruise.
英语要求

英语接近母语级沟通（C1 以上），需高端酒店礼宾或管家经验，服务细节与预判能力要求极高。

English Requirement

Near-native conversational English (C1+); luxury hotel concierge or butler experience preferred, with extremely high standards for detail and anticipation.

🍽️餐饮部Food & Beverage Department小费收入可观团队协作强▼
部门简介

餐饮部是邮轮上与宾客接触最频繁的部门，负责主餐厅、自助餐厅与特色餐厅的全部用餐服务，直接决定宾客对「海上假期」的评价。部门层级清晰：餐厅经理统管全局，领班带班督导，服务生与助理服务生组成服务搭档，自助餐厅服务生保障高峰运转。餐饮岗位以小费文化著称——欧美航线宾客习惯按服务支付小费，服务生的综合收入往往明显高于基础工资，是全船收入弹性最大的部门之一。

Overview

The Food & Beverage Department is the most guest-facing team on board, running the Main Dining Room, buffet and specialty restaurants — it defines the guest dining experience. The hierarchy is clear: the Restaurant Manager oversees the whole operation, Head Waiters lead service teams, Waiters work in pairs with Assistant Waiters, and Buffet Stewards keep high-volume service running smoothly. F&B is famous for its tipping culture — on Western-market ships, tips on top of base salary make wait staff among the highest earners on board.

细分岗位与工作内容 Sub-Positions & Detailed Duties

餐厅经理Restaurant Manager

细分工作内容
  • 汇报对象为酒店总监（Food & Beverage Director），统管全船 2–4 个主餐厅及特色餐厅，负责 30–80 名餐厅员工的排班、培训与绩效考核
  • 每日主持班前会，核对客情、预订、过敏名单与 86 单（售罄菜品）
  • 巡查各服务区域，抽查摆台标准与上菜节奏，处理升级客诉
  • 监控酒水与餐饮营收数据，与厨房部协调菜单与出菜时间
  • 参与宾客满意度复盘，落实船司服务标准的持续改进
Duties
  • Reports to the Food & Beverage Director, managing 2–4 dining venues and 30–80 restaurant staff, including scheduling, training and performance reviews
  • Runs daily pre-shift briefings covering occupancy, reservations, allergy lists and the 86 list
  • Inspects stations for setup and pacing standards and handles escalated guest complaints
  • Monitors food and beverage revenue and coordinates menus and timing with the galley
  • Reviews guest satisfaction scores and drives continuous improvement against brand standards.
薪酬与要求

固定薪资制（管理岗通常无小费），是餐饮部层级最高、责任最重的船上晋升通道；一般要求 3–5 年邮轮餐饮经验或高星级酒店管理经验。

Pay & Requirements

Salaried (management roles usually don't share tips) — the senior F&B step; typically requires 3–5 years of cruise or luxury hotel F&B management experience.

领班Head Waiter

细分工作内容
  • 汇报对象为餐厅经理，每班带领 6–12 名服务生/助理服务生，分管一个 25–40 桌的服务区域
  • 开餐前检查各台位摆台、备品与卫生，主持本区域菜单讲解与 specials 通报
  • 服务高峰期机动支援，处理点单争议、退菜与宾客特殊要求
  • 负责本区域宾客满意度回访与投诉的首次响应
  • 协助餐厅经理对新员工进行带教培训与标准考核
Duties
  • Reports to the Restaurant Manager, leading 6–12 waiters/assistant waiters in a station of 25–40 tables
  • Pre-service inspection of table settings, side stations and cleanliness; briefs the team on menus and specials
  • Moves between tables at peak times to resolve order disputes, returns and special requests
  • First point of contact for guest feedback and complaints in the section
  • Mentors new hires and runs on-the-job training and standards checks.
薪酬与要求

小费采用团队分账制，领班分成比例高于服务生；通常需 2 个以上合同期的服务生经验并英语流利。

Pay & Requirements

Shares in the team tip pool at a higher rate than waiters; usually needs 2+ contracts of waiter experience and fluent English.

服务生Waiter / Waitress

细分工作内容
  • 汇报对象为领班，每餐负责 8–16 张餐桌（约 20–40 位宾客）的全流程服务
  • 餐前 30 分钟到岗完成摆台、备品补充与台位卫生自查
  • 迎宾点单、复述确认并录入 POS，向厨房传达过敏与忌口
  • 按西餐服务规范上菜斟酒，推介酒水与当日 specials，餐中主动回台
  • 餐后收台、清台并重新摆台，保证翻台效率
Duties
  • Reports to the Head Waiter, serving 8–16 tables (about 20–40 guests) per meal period end to end
  • Arrives 30 minutes before service to set tables, restock the side station and inspect the station
  • Greets and seats guests, takes and repeats orders in the POS, and relays allergies to the galley
  • Serves and pours to western service standards, upsells beverages and specials, and checks back during the meal
  • Clears and resets tables after each seating to keep turnaround on time.
薪酬与要求

基础工资较低但小费是主要收入来源（欧美航线综合收入全船前列）；英语需达到日常点单与推介的流利水平。

Pay & Requirements

Low base salary with tips as the main income (top-tier overall earnings on Western-market ships); requires fluent working English for order taking and upselling.

助理服务生Assistant Waiter

细分工作内容
  • 汇报对象为其搭档服务生与领班，与一名服务生搭档负责同一组桌位（通常 8–12 桌）
  • 开餐前协助摆台、折叠口布、补充面包篮与餐具，确保台位达标
  • 餐中负责递送面包与黄油、添加饮品、撤盘换碟，衔接厨房出菜与传菜
  • 协助推介酒水与甜品（推介成功计入团队业绩）
  • 收台后与搭档共同清台、重新摆台，为下一餐做准备
Duties
  • Reports to the assigned Waiter and the Head Waiter, teamed with one waiter on a shared section of 8–12 tables
  • Helps set tables, folds napkins, restocks bread baskets and cutlery before service
  • During service, serves bread and butter, refills drinks, clears plates and runs food from the galley
  • Assists with beverage and dessert upselling (counts toward team sales)
  • Clears and resets the section with the waiter to be ready for the next seating.
薪酬与要求

小费按较低比例分成，是餐饮部标准入门岗；零经验或 1 年以下餐饮经验可入，英语能完成基础服务对话即可。

Pay & Requirements

Shares tips at an entry-level rate — the standard entry role in restaurant service; open to candidates with no or under 1 year of F&B experience and basic service English.

自助餐厅服务生Buffet Steward

细分工作内容
  • 汇报对象为自助餐厅主管（Buffet Supervisor/Café Team Leader），负责自助餐厅指定区域（如热菜台、甜品台）
  • 每餐前补齐餐台菜品、更换餐夹、整理餐盘与饮品站
  • 高峰时段（早餐可达数千人次进餐）持续巡台，及时补菜、清理泼洒与归位餐具
  • 引导宾客排队与取餐，维持餐台秩序与整洁
  • 闭餐后协助分拣餐具送洗碗间，清洁餐台与区域地面
Duties
  • Reports to the Buffet Supervisor, owning a section of the buffet such as the hot line or dessert station
  • Restocks dishes, changes serving utensils and keeps plates and beverage stations tidy before each meal
  • Circulates constantly during peak service (breakfast can serve thousands) to replenish food, wipe spills and reset cutlery
  • Guides queue flow and keeps the buffet line orderly and clean
  • After service, sorts dishware for the scullery and cleans the line and surrounding floors.
薪酬与要求

以固定薪资为主、小费占比低，胜在节奏简单、对英语要求低；零经验可入，是餐饮部最常见的起点岗之一。

Pay & Requirements

Mostly fixed salary with little tip share, in exchange for simpler duties and low English requirements; no experience needed — one of the most common entry points in F&B.

👨‍🍳厨房部 GalleyGalley / Culinary Department专业技能强证书经验优先▼
部门简介

厨房部（船上惯称 Galley）负责全船宾客与船员的三餐及宴会出品，是大邮轮上人数最多的部门之一——大型邮轮 Galley 通常有 100–200 名厨师与管事员工，每日出餐上万份。层级沿用法式厨房体系：行政总厨统筹、副行政总厨分管、厨师主管（Chef de Partie）负责各自台位、初级厨师（Commis）执行与学习，管事部（Utility/Scullery）承担清洁与洗碗。厨艺岗位看重证书与实战经验，晋升按「台位—分管—总厨」路线清晰推进。

Overview

The Galley produces every meal for guests and crew, from main dining rooms to banquets — one of the largest departments on a big ship, with 100–200 cooks and utility staff serving thousands of covers a day. It follows the classical brigade system: the Executive Chef runs the galley, Sous Chefs manage sections, Chef de Parties own their stations, Commis cook and learn, and the Utility/Scullery team keeps everything clean. Culinary ranks value certificates and hands-on experience, with a clear path from station to management.

细分岗位与工作内容 Sub-Positions & Detailed Duties

行政总厨Executive Chef

细分工作内容
  • 汇报对象为酒店总监（Hotel Director/F&B Director 体系），统管全船 Galley 及 100–200 名厨房员工
  • 制定每周菜单与特色餐厅菜单，控制食材成本与库存（成本率通常须控制在营收的既定比例内）
  • 主持厨房班前会与菜品品控，抽查各台位出品标准与摆盘规范
  • 负责食品安全体系（HACCP）在 Galley 的落实与内审迎检
  • 与采购、餐饮部协调补货计划，处理重大宴会（船长晚宴常达数百席）的出品统筹
Duties
  • Reports to the Hotel Director through the F&B structure, running the entire galley and its 100–200 culinary staff
  • Plans weekly and specialty-restaurant menus, controlling food cost and inventory within target ratios
  • Chairs galley line-ups and quality checks, auditing plating and production standards across stations
  • Owns HACCP food-safety compliance and internal audits in the galley
  • Coordinates provisioning with purchasing and F&B, and leads production for major events such as captain's dinners of several hundred covers.
薪酬与要求

固定薪资为厨房部最高档；通常要求 culinary 学历、多年豪华酒店/邮轮厨房管理经验及食品卫生类证书（如 HACCP/Level 4 Food Safety）。

Pay & Requirements

Top fixed salary in the galley; typically requires a culinary qualification, years of luxury hotel/cruise kitchen management experience, and food-safety credentials such as HACCP or Level 4 Food Safety.

副行政总厨Sous Chef

细分工作内容
  • 汇报对象为行政总厨，分管 1–2 个厨房区域（如主餐厅热厨、冷厨 Garde Manger 或特色餐厅厨房），带领 15–40 名厨师
  • 按菜单拆解备餐任务，监督各台位 mise en place（备料）数量与完成时间
  • 开餐时段站在出菜口把控出品质量、摆盘与出菜节奏
  • 在行政总厨离岗时代行其管理职责
  • 负责本区域食品安全温度记录与卫生巡检
Duties
  • Reports to the Executive Chef, managing one or two galley sections (main galley hot line, Garde Manger or a specialty venue) with 15–40 cooks
  • Breaks menus into prep tasks and supervises mise en place quantities and completion times
  • Works the pass during service, checking quality, plating and course pacing
  • Acts for the Executive Chef when off duty
  • Keeps temperature logs and hygiene inspections current for the section.
薪酬与要求

固定薪资，管理岗层级；一般要求 3–5 年厨房经验（含 1 年以上主管经验）与中级以上厨师证书，英语需能与多国厨师沟通。

Pay & Requirements

Fixed salary at management level; usually needs 3–5 years in kitchens including 1+ year supervisory experience, an intermediate culinary certificate, and English sufficient to work with a multinational brigade.

厨师主管Chef de Partie (CDP)

细分工作内容
  • 汇报对象为副行政总厨，独立负责一个台位（如酱汁台 Sauce、烧烤台 Grill、冷台 Garde Manger、面点台 Pastry），带 2–6 名初级厨师
  • 按当日预订人数完成本台位 mise en place，确保备料量与开餐时间匹配
  • 开餐时主导本台位出品，保证每道菜按配方、温度与摆盘标准执行
  • 培训与检查本台 Commis 的刀工、烹饪与卫生操作
  • 控制本台位食材损耗，按规定填写库存与损耗记录
Duties
  • Reports to the Sous Chef, owning one station (Sauce, Grill, Garde Manger, Pastry, etc.) with 2–6 Commis
  • Completes mise en place for the station based on reservation counts and service times
  • Leads production at the station during service, holding every dish to recipe, temperature and plating standards
  • Trains and checks Commis on knife skills, cooking methods and hygiene
  • Controls station-level waste and keeps stock and wastage records up to date.
薪酬与要求

薪资与船司小费政策相关，多数按固定薪资计；通常要求 2–3 年对应台位经验与中级厨师证（中式台位多要求中餐经验）。

Pay & Requirements

Paid on fixed salary under most cruise line policies; typically 2–3 years of experience on the relevant station with an intermediate culinary certificate (Chinese-cuisine stations usually require wok-line experience).

初级厨师Commis (Commis Chef)

细分工作内容
  • 汇报对象为其所在台位的 Chef de Partie，每餐跟随 CDP 完成备料与出品
  • 负责洗切配、剥洗蔬菜、调制基础酱汁与批量预制（每日处理食材常以数十至上百公斤计）
  • 按标准配方协助完成汤品、配菜与简单主菜的烹制
  • 保持本台位清洁，执行生熟分开、标签与保质期管理等食品安全规范
  • 参加厨房技能培训，向上一级台位晋升
Duties
  • Reports to the Chef de Partie of the assigned station, supporting prep and production every service
  • Handles washing, cutting and batch prep — often processing tens to over a hundred kilograms of ingredients daily
  • Cooks soups, sides and simple mains to standard recipes under supervision
  • Keeps the station clean and follows food-safety rules such as raw/cooked separation, labeling and FIFO stock rotation
  • Attends culinary training and works toward the next station up the brigade.
薪酬与要求

固定薪资，是厨师线入门岗；一般要求初级厨师证或职校烹饪学历，零船上经验可入，英语能看懂配方与安全标识即可。

Pay & Requirements

Fixed salary — the entry rung of the cooking line; usually an entry-level culinary certificate or culinary schooling, no shipboard experience needed, and English enough to read recipes and safety labels.

管事部员（洗碗工）Galley Utility / Dishwasher

细分工作内容
  • 汇报对象因船司而异：管事部主管（Utility Supervisor / Galley Steward 体系），部分船司设卫生主管（Sanitation Officer / Sanitation Supervisor）直接管理洗碗与消毒班组
  • 操作大型洗碗机清洗锅具、餐具与餐盘（高峰期每餐清洗量可达数千件），并按 USPH/FDA/SEMS 规范记录三星槽（3-sink system）与洗碗机水温
  • 手工清洁大型锅具、烤盘与厨房设备，按规范分类堆放
  • 清洁油污分离器（grease trap），垃圾按 MARPOL 规范分拣后送垃圾房冷冻储存
  • 负责厨房地面、排水沟清洁消毒，按班表参加 USPH 检查区域清洁
  • 协助食材搬运、库房整理、靠港日帮工（行李搬运/舷梯值岗）与厨房移位清洁（deep cleaning）
Duties
  • Reports to the Utility Supervisor under the Galley Steward structure — on several lines a Sanitation Officer/Sanitation Supervisor directly oversees dishwashing and sanitation teams
  • Operates industrial dish machines for pots, pans and tableware — peak service can mean thousands of items per meal — logging 3-sink system and machine temperatures per USPH/FDA/SEMS
  • Hand-scrubs large pots, trays and kitchen equipment and stacks them to standard
  • Cleans grease traps and sorts garbage per MARPOL rules for refrigerated waste storage
  • Cleans and sanitizes floors and drains, and joins USPH inspection prep on assigned areas
  • Helps with food deliveries, storeroom upkeep, gangway/luggage duties on port days and scheduled deep cleaning.
薪酬与要求

月薪约 USD 600–900、无小费，周工时可达 70 小时，是船上薪资最低、工作强度最大的岗位之一；无经验无证书要求，英语基础即可，体力好、能接受高温潮湿环境。虽然冷门，但它是零基础进入船员队伍的常见起点——表现好可在 1–2 个合同内晋升初级厨师（Commis / Third Cook）转烹饪线。

Pay & Requirements

Around USD 600–900/month with no tips and up to 70-hour weeks — among the lowest-paid, hardest-working roles on board. No certificates or experience needed and only basic English, but requires stamina and tolerance for a hot, wet environment. A proven stepping stone: strong performers are often promoted to Commis / Third Cook within one or two contracts.

过敏源全流程实操规范 Allergen Handling Procedure

  1. 接单标记（Allergy Order Flag）：点单系统亮起过敏标记（allergy flag）后，须由厨师长与主管双人复核，确认过敏源类别并核对桌号与菜单，杜绝漏看。 / When an order prints with an allergy flag, the chef and supervisor must double-verify the allergen, table number and menu items before prep begins.
  2. 器具隔离（Dedicated Equipment）：使用独立锅具、刀具与砧板，按船司分色规范启用专用砧板（常见紫色/红色），绝不与普通出品混用。 / Use dedicated pots, knives and cutting boards — commonly purple or red boards per line policy — never shared with regular production.
  3. 环境净化（Sanitize Workstation）：操作前洗手并更换一次性手套，彻底清洁并消毒台面、层架与邻近设备，清除残留过敏原。 / Wash hands, change gloves, and fully clean and sanitize the workstation, shelving and nearby equipment to remove allergen residue.
  4. 独立出餐（Separate Plating & Pass）：使用专用传盘并加盖罩，票单标注 Allergy Ticket；由厨师长（Chef de Partie/Sous Chef）亲手完成摆盘与放行。 / Plate on a dedicated tray with a cover, flag the allergy ticket, and have the chef personally plate and release the dish.
  5. 双向确认（Double Confirmation）：厨师长与传菜员口头复述桌号与过敏源，上桌时明确告知宾客「本菜品不含该过敏源」。 / The chef and food runner verbally repeat the table number and allergen, and the server confirms the allergen-free dish with the guest at the table.
🚨 过敏事故零容忍：一次交叉接触（cross-contact）可能引发过敏性休克（anaphylaxis）并危及生命。任何环节存疑——疑似污染、标签不清、器具混用——一律整单重做，不得凭经验放行。Zero tolerance for allergen incidents: a single cross-contact can cause fatal anaphylaxis. If anything is in doubt — suspected contamination, unclear labeling, shared equipment — remake the entire order. Never guess.

部门简介

酒吧部统管全船所有酒水点位的运营，包括主泳池吧、大堂吧、特色酒吧（如马天尼吧、威士忌吧）以及 Casino 吧，负责酒水出品、销售与吧台卫生，是船上现金流最活跃的部门之一。编制上通常设酒吧经理（Bar Manager）之下有领班、调酒师与酒吧服务生，层级清晰、晋升通道明确。该部门对英语口语要求较高——每天面对大量欧美宾客点单与闲聊，也对性格有明确偏好：外向、健谈、抗压能力强的人更容易拿高小费、快速晋升。

Overview

The Bar Department runs every beverage outlet onboard — the main pool bar, lobby bar, specialty bars such as the Martini and Whiskey Bar, and the Casino bar — covering drink production, sales and bar sanitation. It is one of the highest-cash-flow departments on any cruise ship. Under the Bar Manager, the team typically consists of bar team leaders, bartenders and bar waiters, with a clear hierarchy and promotion path. Strong spoken English is essential, as crew interact constantly with international guests; an outgoing, talkative and resilient personality is highly valued and directly linked to tip income and career growth.

调酒师Bartender

细分工作内容
  • 向酒吧领班（Bar Team Leader）汇报，被分配驻守主泳池吧或特色酒吧一个固定吧位，旺季高峰期一晚出品可达 150–250 杯鸡尾酒。

  • Reports to the Bar Team Leader and is assigned to a fixed station at the main pool bar or a specialty bar; at peak times a bartender can produce 150–250 cocktails in one evening.

部门简介

娱乐部负责全船宾客的日常活动与晚间演出，是营造「船上就是度假目的地」氛围的核心部门。工作内容涵盖活动主持、甲板游戏、儿童俱乐部、舞台演出与灯光音响技术支持。该部门偏爱性格外向、精力充沛、有才艺或教育背景的候选人，英语口语需达到能持续主持和与多国宾客互动的水平。合同期通常 6–8 个月，工作多在白天与晚间，作息不规律但氛围轻松。

Overview

The Entertainment Department creates the "ship as destination" experience through daily activities, evening shows, kids' clubs, and technical show support. Contracts typically run 6–8 months, with irregular day-and-evening hours in a fun, fast-paced environment.

部门简介

Overview

The Onboard Revenue Division runs duty-free shops, boutiques, and photo galleries selling fragrances, watches, jewelry, liquor, tobacco, and souvenirs. Pay is typically base salary plus sales commission — for strong sellers, commission can substantially exceed the base (ratios vary by cruise line) — making it one of the highest-earning departments for crew. Shop hours shift between port days and sea days, with rotating shifts during peak port-day traffic. Fluent English and retail sales experience are standard requirements; some lines also value Mandarin for Chinese guest markets.

销售话术与技巧 Selling Techniques

迎宾不压迫：开门见山表达「随时为您服务」，给宾客 1–2 分钟自由浏览空间，再回来接续话题，是免税店高端接待的第一步。"Welcome! Feel free to browse — let me know if you have any questions. By the way, we just received the new summer collection."
需求挖掘：用开放式问题（open questions）探明用途、预算与偏好——是自用还是送礼、送给谁、平时什么风格，再收窄推荐范围。"May I ask — is this a gift or something for yourself? And who would it be for? That'll help me point you to exactly the right piece."
FAB 推介法：按「特点 Feature → 优势 Advantage → 利益 Benefit」三层展开，把产品参数翻译成宾客能感受到的价值。"This watch is automatic — no battery ever needed (feature), so it keeps perfect time without maintenance (advantage) — meaning you can wear it every day for years and it becomes an heirloom, not just an accessory (benefit)."
限时限购营造：用真实的港口日专享折扣与截止时间制造合理紧迫感，只说事实不施压，让宾客自己决定「现在买更划算」。"Just so you know — this port day special ends tonight at midnight, and these sets are limited to two per guest. If you're considering it, tonight is the best time."
连带销售：围绕主商品推荐成套搭配（matching items）——买了香水推同款身体乳，买了腕表推表带或首饰，成交率自然提升。"If you love this fragrance, the matching body lotion makes the scent last so much longer — and right now the duo set saves you 15%. Shall I wrap them together?"
💡 免税合规提示：免税店必须在船舶进入公海（International Waters）后 方可开业销售，进入领海（Territorial Waters）前须关店封存；烟酒等受限品类按宾客航程目的地海关额度销售，收银时必须核对宾客航次、护照与限购额度，超限不予放行。Duty-free compliance: shops may only trade in international waters and must close before entering territorial waters; liquor and tobacco are subject to customs allowances — cashiers must verify each guest's voyage and allowance before releasing restricted items.

部门简介

安保与医务是全船安全与健康的保障部门：安保团队执行 SOLAS 与 ISPS 规则下的门禁管控、巡逻、监控与应急演练，医务团队负责宾客与船员的诊疗、急救与健康证书管理。两类岗位均要求持证上岗（安保需相关安保/海事培训，医务需护理或医师执业资格），英语需达到能在应急场景下清晰下达与执行指令的水平。工作实行轮班值守，夜间与港口日为重点时段。

Overview

Security & Medical safeguards the safety and health of everyone on board. The Security team enforces access control, patrols, CCTV monitoring, and drills under SOLAS and the ISPS Code, while the Medical team provides clinical care, emergency response, and medical certificate management for guests and crew. Both require valid credentials — maritime/security training for Security, nursing or physician licenses for Medical — and English strong enough to give and follow orders clearly in emergencies. Staff work rotating watches, with nights and port days as peak periods.

细分岗位与工作内容 Sub-Positions & Detailed Duties

安保员Security Guard

细分工作内容
  • 执行舷梯口与船员通道的门禁管控，核验船卡与访客证件，每班查验数百人次
  • 按固定路线巡船（通常每 2–4 小时一轮），覆盖客舱区、机舱入口与仓储区
  • 监控 CCTV 墙并记录异常，发现可疑行为立即上报值班安保官
  • 参与每周弃船、消防、防海盗等演练，熟悉集合站与应急装备位置
  • 掌握常用应急双语口令（示例见下）。语言要求：能听懂英文指令并做基础英文沟通与记录
Duties
  • Control access at gangways and crew passages, verifying crew cards and visitor IDs — often several hundred checks per shift
  • Patrol assigned routes (typically every 2–4 hours) covering stateroom areas, engine-room entrances, and storerooms
  • Monitor CCTV walls, log anomalies, and report suspicious behavior to the Security Officer on watch immediately
  • Join weekly abandon-ship, fire, and anti-piracy drills and know muster stations and emergency gear locations
  • Master standard emergency phrases in English (examples below). Language: able to understand English orders and communicate and log basic information in English.

Emergency Phrases
  • "Man overboard!" — shout loudly and report to the bridge the moment someone falls into the water
  • "Fire, fire! Fire on Deck 5. This is not a drill." — the standard fire report, using "this is not a drill" to demand immediate attention.

安保官Security Officer

细分工作内容
  • 担任轮值安全值班官，指挥安保员执行巡逻与门禁任务，向安保经理汇报
  • 掌握 SOLAS 与 ISPS 规则要点：船舶保安等级（Security Level 1–3）调整、限制区域管理、登船审批与《保安声明》流程
  • 处置宾客纠纷、醉酒、失窃与骚扰类事件，制作英文事件报告（Incident Report）
  • 组织与记录每周安全演练，配合 ISPS 内外审与港口国检查（PSC）
  • 语言要求：英语流利，能独立完成英文报告、检查问答与应急指挥
Duties
  • Serve as duty Security Officer, directing guards on patrol and access control and reporting to the Security Manager
  • Apply SOLAS and ISPS Code fundamentals: adjusting ship security levels (Level 1–3), managing restricted areas, approving embarkation, and handling the Declaration of Security
  • Resolve guest incidents — disputes, intoxication, theft, harassment — and write Incident Reports in English
  • Organize and document weekly drills, supporting ISPS internal/external audits and Port State Control inspections
  • Language: fluent English for reporting, inspection Q&A, and emergency command.
应急口令示例
  • "Abandon ship! Proceed to your muster station." —— 弃船指令，要求人员按分配前往集合站
  • "Security alert! All personnel report to your emergency stations." —— 保安事件下的集结口令，与弃船口令不可混用
Emergency Phrases
  • "Abandon ship! Proceed to your muster station." — the abandon-ship order directing personnel to their assigned stations
  • "Security alert! All personnel report to your emergency stations." — the assembly call for a security incident; keep it distinct from abandon-ship commands.

船上护士Ship Nurse

细分工作内容
  • 在船医指导下完成门诊接诊、换药、输液与用药，日均接诊量因航季约 5–20 人次
  • 执行急救响应：与医疗团队携带急救装备赶赴现场，实施心肺复苏、止血包扎等初级处置
  • 管理药品与医疗耗材库存，执行效期检查与双人核对
  • 负责船员健康证书、体检安排与传染病防控（如胃肠道疫情隔离流程）
  • 硬性要求：注册护士（RN）证书 + 至少 2–3 年临床经验，多数船司要求具备急救/ICU/急诊背景，英语流利
Duties
  • Provide outpatient care — dressings, IV therapy, and medication — under the ship doctor's direction, typically 5–20 visits per day depending on the season
  • Respond to medical emergencies with the medical team, carrying the response kit and delivering first-line care such as CPR and bleeding control
  • Manage pharmaceutical and supplies inventory with expiry checks and double verification
  • Oversee crew medical certificates, physicals, and infection control (e.g., gastrointestinal outbreak isolation procedures)
  • Requirements: a registered Nurse (RN) license plus 2–3 years of clinical experience, ideally in emergency, ICU, or acute care, with fluent English.

船医Ship Doctor

细分工作内容
  • 独立完成全船宾客与船员的诊疗决策，涵盖内科急症、外伤缝合、慢病管理等，并对转岸就医做出判断
  • 主导重大急救（心梗、卒中、严重外伤），指挥医疗团队并联系岸上医疗支持（远程会诊）
  • 签发死亡证明、医疗不适航证明等法定文件，配合港口卫生检疫
  • 管理医务室运营、医疗物资采购计划与团队排班
  • 硬性要求：执业医师资格 + 至少 3–5 年临床经验（外科/急救优先），多数船司要求急救技能证书（如 ACLS），英语流利
Duties
  • Make independent clinical decisions for all guests and crew — acute medical conditions, wound suturing, chronic disease management — and decide when shore-side evacuation is needed
  • Lead major emergencies (heart attack, stroke, major trauma), directing the medical team and arranging telemedical support from shore
  • Issue statutory documents such as death certificates and unfit-to-travel notices, coordinating with port health authorities
  • Manage the medical center's operations, supply ordering, and team scheduling
  • Requirements: a valid medical license plus 3–5 years of clinical experience (surgery or emergency care preferred), advanced life support certification (e.g., ACLS) per most lines, and fluent English.

部门简介

娱乐场是船上营收贡献最大的部门之一，运营赌台（Blackjack、Roulette 等）、老虎机与收银柜台，服务全球高消费客群。所有赌台岗位必须完成船司自办的荷官学校（Dealer School）培训并通过考核才能上岗，对英语口语、手眼协调与操作规范要求极高。特别提醒：娱乐场在中国领海内及船舶停靠期间必须停止营业（not in operation within territorial waters / while alongside），员工需配合随时调整营业时段与排班。

Overview

The Casino is one of the highest-revenue departments on board, running gaming tables (Blackjack, Roulette and more), slot machines and the cashier's cage for high-spending guests. Every table-games position requires completing the line's own Dealer School and passing its certification before dealing, with demanding standards for spoken English, hand-eye coordination and procedure. Note: the casino must close — it is not in operation within territorial waters or while alongside — so staff adjust operating hours and rosters accordingly.

💡 旺季提示：地中海与阿拉斯加航季的港口日，观光部常在开船前数小时仍在码头处理宾客延误与改签——「all aboard time 前的最后人数核对」是每天最重要的一项纪律，务必与码头协调员、前台三方核对无误后才能开航。On Mediterranean and Alaska port days, excursions teams often work the pier until the last hour before sailing — the final headcount before the all aboard time, cross-checked with the tender coordinator and Guest Services, is the day's most critical routine.
💰财务部Finance / Purser's Department数字敏感后台岗位▼
部门简介

财务部（船上传统称「事务长部」Purser's Department）是全船资金与账务的中枢，负责宾客账户、船上营收结算、现金与外汇管理及船员薪资发放。与前台共用宾客账单（folio）数据但更偏后台：大量工作是与岸上财务总部对账、出报表、做审计留痕。团队规模小（大型邮轮通常 5–10 人），要求算数精准、诚信记录无瑕、能用英语完成报表与往来邮件，是财务背景候选人进入邮轮行业的对口通道。

Overview

Finance — traditionally the Purser's Department — is the ship's money hub, managing guest accounts, onboard revenue settlement, cash and foreign exchange, and crew payroll. It works with guest folio data alongside Guest Services but is mostly back-of-house: reconciling with the shore-side finance office, producing reports and keeping audit trails. Small teams (typically 5–10 on large ships) demand precision, an impeccable integrity record and English strong enough for reports and correspondence — a natural fit for finance graduates.

  • Manage shipboard cash and FX holdings, arranging port-day cash replenishment and bank deliveries

Finance or accounting background, or 1+ year of cashiering/finance experience; detail-oriented, Excel-proficient, with strong written English.

薪资核算或会计经验，细致耐心；英语需能向多国籍船员解释工资构成。

Payroll or accounting experience with strong attention to detail; English able to explain pay structures to a multinational crew.

👥人力资源部Human Resources / Crew Management船员之家沟通协调▼
部门简介

人力资源部（船上常称船员部 Crew Management）被称为「船员之家」，服务对象不是宾客而是全船数百至上千名船员：负责船员签约与上下船行程、培训与证书跟踪、绩效与纪律程序、宿舍膳食与船员活动。它是 MLC 2006（海事劳工公约）在船上的落地执行者，需平衡总部政策与多国籍船员的实际需求。团队通常 3–8 人，要求沟通能力强、英语流利、熟悉海事劳工法规，是具有 HR 或海员服务背景候选人最对口的部门。

Overview

HR — often called Crew Management on board — is the "crew family" office: its guests are the hundreds to thousands of crew, whose sign-on/sign-off travel, training and certificates, performance and disciplinary procedures, accommodation, meals and welfare events all run through this team. It puts MLC 2006 (the Maritime Labour Convention) into practice on board, balancing head-office policy with a multinational crew's real needs. Small teams of 3–8 need strong interpersonal skills, fluent English and solid maritime labour-law knowledge — the natural home for HR or seafarer-services backgrounds.

  • 维护船员花名册与舱位分配，处理宿舍调换申请
  • 对接各国港口移民与海关，办理船员登陆许可（shore pass）与证件申报
  • 管理船员档案的完整性与保密性，配合总部人力系统数据更新
Duties

  • Organize sign-on and sign-off: contracts, medicals, document checks and filing
  • Plan crew rotations, arranging flights, hotels and port transfers

  • Liaise with port immigration and customs for shore passes and crew manifest declarations
  • Keep crew records complete and confidential, syncing data with head-office HR systems.
入门要求

♻️环境保护与设备维修Environmental & Facility Maintenance小众稳定技术门槛中▼
部门简介

环境保护与设备维修是邮轮上默默运转的「市政部门」，承担两大职能：一是 MARPOL 环保合规——垃圾分类、污水处理、油水分离与合法送岸处置，向船长直接负责；二是船舶酒店区设备的日常维修保养，与轮机部协作保障客舱与公共区域正常运转。岗位招聘量小但需求稳定，多数要求动手技能或环保/工科背景，适合有技术专长、追求长期合同的候选人。

Overview

This small but steady "municipal services" team quietly keeps the ship running. It covers two functions: MARPOL environmental compliance — waste segregation, wastewater treatment, oily water separation and legal shore-side disposal, reporting directly to the Master — and day-to-day maintenance of hotel-area equipment, working alongside the engine department to keep staterooms and public areas in service. Openings are few but consistent; most roles require hands-on trade skills or an environmental/engineering background, suiting candidates with technical expertise seeking longer contracts.

细分工作内容
  • 直接向船长汇报 MARPOL 环保合规（MSC 官方招聘确认），是《垃圾管理计划》（Garbage Management Plan）指定责任人
  • 监督全船垃圾分类、标识、储存与靠港合法送岸处置，编制垃圾送岸通知（Waste Notification）
  • 管理污水处理、油水分离器（15ppm 标准）、焚烧炉运行计划与取样检测记录
  • 对全员开展环保与化学品安全培训，配合安全官组织污染应急演练
  • 保存全部环保日志（Garbage Record Book 等），配合内外部审核检查
Duties
  • Reports directly to the Master on MARPOL compliance (confirmed by MSC's official postings) and acts as the designated person for the Garbage Management Plan
  • Oversees waste segregation, labeling, storage and legal shore-side disposal, preparing waste notifications for ports
  • Manages wastewater treatment, the 15ppm oily water separator, incinerator schedules and sampling records
  • Delivers environmental and chemical-safety training and runs pollution drills with the Safety Officer
  • Keeps all environmental logbooks (including the Garbage Record Book) ready for internal and external audits.

  • 区分可回收物、餐厨垃圾、塑料与危废并按 MARPOL 附则 V 规范记入垃圾记录簿

  • 保障垃圾在焚烧、处理或送岸前的正确存放与交接

  • Reports to the Environmental Officer and handles day-to-day segregation and transfer at all collection points
  • Operates compactors, sorting stations and refrigerated waste storage while keeping waste rooms clean
  • Separates recyclables, food waste, plastics and hazardous waste with entries in the Garbage Record Book under MARPOL Annex V
  • Assists with cleaning around grey/black water system areas
  • Ensures proper storage and handover before incineration, treatment or shore-side landing.

酒店维修技师（修理工）Hotel Fitter / Facility Maintenance Technician

细分工作内容
  • 隶属酒店技术维修团队（Hotel Technical，与轮机部协作，BSM 等船管公司招聘确认）

  • 执行预防性维护巡检，配合翻新改造项目施工
  • 严守工作安全规程，完工后清理现场并提交工单报告

  • Joins the hotel technical maintenance team (coordinating with the engine department; confirmed by ship managers such as BSM)

  • Installs or replaces light fixtures, furniture, door locks and hardware, closing hotel maintenance tickets
  • Performs routine preventive inspections and supports refurbishment projects
  • Follows safety procedures, cleans the work space and reports completed work.
