英文自我介绍脚本English Self-Introduction Script (60–90 sec)

使用说明

结构：问候 → 基本信息 → 经验与成绩 → 应聘动机 → 结尾。语速放慢、微笑注视面试官，背熟但不背僵。

Script

Good morning. My name is [Name], from [City], China. I majored in [Major] at [College]. For the past [X] years, I have worked as a [Position] at [Company], where I [key achievement, e.g. served 80+ guests daily and won "Server of the Month"]. I'm applying for the [Position] role because I want to build a career in international hospitality, and I'm ready for the challenge of shipboard life. I'm hardworking, adaptable, and I genuinely enjoy taking care of guests. Thank you for this opportunity.

英文求职信模板Cover Letter Template

使用说明

邮轮应聘邮件正文即可充当求职信，控制在 150 词内：应聘岗位 + 三个匹配点 + 可到岗时间 + 证件状态。附件命名规范：WangLei-Waiter-CV.pdf。

Template

Dear Hiring Team,

I am writing to apply for the [Position] position. With [X] years of experience in [field], I have developed strong skills in [point 1] and [point 2]. I hold valid [STCW / seafarer's medical certificate] and am available to embark from [date].

My CV is attached for your review. Thank you for your consideration, and I look forward to the opportunity of an interview.

Best regards,
[Name] | [Phone] | [Email]

简历常见错误 Top 10Top 10 CV Mistakes to Avoid

  1. 超过 1 页——船员简历黄金标准就是 1 页Over one page — one page is the gold standard
  2. 缺少正装免冠照Missing a formal headshot
  3. 英语描述直接机翻（如把「服务员」直译成 waiter，或将工作描述写成中式长句）Machine-translated English job descriptions
  4. 写家庭住址、身份证号等无关隐私信息Irrelevant private details (home address, ID number)
  5. 只列职责不列成绩（应写「服务 80+ 宾客/日」而非「负责点单」）Listing duties instead of measurable achievements
  6. 英语水平写「良好」却无任何证明Claiming "good English" without proof
  7. 邮箱用昵称（应使用姓名拼音邮箱）Unprofessional email address
  8. 附件名乱（应为 姓名-岗位-CV.pdf）Messy attachment file name
  9. 隐瞒工作空窗期或频繁跳槽原因（面试必被问）Hidden gaps or unexplained job hopping
  10. 照片用自拍/生活照/过度美颜Casual or heavily filtered photos

求职材料清单Application Document Checklist

  • 中英双语简历（1 页，附正装照）Bilingual CV, 1 page with formal headshot
  • 护照首页扫描件（有效期 > 合同期 + 6 个月）Passport bio page (validity: contract + 6 months)
  • 学历证书与成绩单（部分船司要求公证/认证）Diploma & transcripts (notarized if required)
  • 英语能力证明（CET / IELTS / TOEFL，如有）English proficiency proof if available
  • 相关培训证书（STCW、职业资格证，如有）Training certificates (STCW, vocational) if obtained
  • 2 寸白底正装免冠照 + 全身照（部分中介要求）Formal photos: white background headshot & full-body
⚠️ 通用参考声明：模板内容为通用参考，请根据个人真实情况修改，切勿虚构工作经验与证书。Templates are for reference — customize with your real information. Never fabricate experience or certificates.
