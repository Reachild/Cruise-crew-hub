客房管家Stateroom Attendant / Cabin Steward

隶属客房部（Housekeeping），负责客舱清洁、布草更换与宾客日常服务，是宾客每天都会接触的「贴身管家」，也是全船用工量最大的一线岗位。

客房部 英语要求：中 用工量最大

岗位概览Position Overview

中文

客房管家负责客舱的日常清洁与保养、布草与毛巾更换、卫浴消毒、消耗品补充以及夜间开床服务，同时记录宾客习惯与特殊需求，为宾客提供「宾至如归」的住宿体验。该岗位体力要求较高、需长时间站立行走，但英语门槛相对较低，招聘名额全船最多，是新人上船最常见的切入点。合同期通常为 6–8 个月，工作日基本无休，靠港日照常打扫。

English

The Stateroom Attendant is responsible for daily stateroom cleaning and upkeep, linen and towel replacement, bathroom sanitizing, amenity replenishment, and evening turndown service, while logging guest preferences and special requests to deliver a true "home away from home" experience. The role is physically demanding with long hours on foot, but the English requirement is moderate and it offers the largest number of openings on board — making it the most common entry point for new crew. Contracts typically run 6–8 months, with cleaning duties continuing on port days.

日常工作流程 SOPStandard Operating Procedure

1客舱晨间清洁 Morning Stateroom Cleaning
中文

敲门三下并自报身份，确认无人后方可进入 → 拉开窗帘（内舱调新风）、收走垃圾与用过的布草 → 按序清洁：先卫浴、后卧室 → 铺床使用「三线对齐」标准，床品平整无毛发污渍 → 补齐配额备品，最后喷洒空气清新剂并关闭电源检查。每间标准清洁时间约 20–30 分钟。

English

Knock three times and announce yourself; enter only after confirming the cabin is vacant → open the drapes, remove trash and used linen → clean in sequence: bathroom first, then the sleeping area → make the bed with straight, even lines and no hair or stains → replenish amenities to par levels, finish with air freshener and a final power check. Allow roughly 20–30 minutes per stateroom.

2布草与毛巾更换 / 毛巾艺术 Linen & Towel Change / Towel Art
中文

执行布草环保计划：浴巾、手巾按宾客悬挂状态判断是否更换（悬挂=重复使用，落地=更换）→ 撤下的布草分色分袋送洗衣房 → 按当日配额领取新布草并登记数量 → 在床铺或梳妆台制作毛巾艺术（天鹅、大象、猴子等），摆放迎宾卡，为宾客留下惊喜。

English

Follow the linen reuse program: replace bath and hand towels only if they are on the floor (hanging towels are reused) → sort removed linen by color into separate bags for the laundry → collect fresh linen against the daily par count and record quantities → fold towel animals (swan, elephant, monkey, etc.) on the bed or vanity with a welcome card to delight the guests.

3卫浴消毒 Bathroom Sanitizing
中文

佩戴手套，按「从上到下、从干净到脏」顺序操作：镜面 → 洗手台 → 浴缸/淋浴间 → 马桶（最后处理）→ 使用船司指定消毒剂浸泡马桶座圈、把手与水龙头 → 更换马桶圈消毒条与地巾 → 补齐洗手液、洗发水、沐浴露至配额，最后检查无水渍、无毛发、无异味。

English

Wear gloves and work top-down from cleanest to dirtiest: mirror → vanity → bathtub/shower → the toilet (save it for last) → soak the toilet seat, handles and faucets with the approved disinfectant → replace the toilet sanitation strip and bath mat → replenish soap, shampoo and body gel to par levels, then check for water spots, hair and odors.

4公共区域与走廊 Public Areas & Corridors
中文

每日对所负责楼层走廊进行吸尘、除渍与边角清洁 → 擦拭楼层指示牌、消防设备玻璃与电梯门 → 及时清理送餐餐车与遗留托盘 → 客房车停靠靠墙并摆放整齐，不得堵塞消防通道 → 发现地毯污渍或照明故障立即报修并跟进闭环。

English

Vacuum corridors, remove stains and clean edges daily → wipe deck signage, fire equipment cabinets and elevator doors → clear room service trolleys and stray trays promptly → park housekeeping carts neatly against the wall without blocking escape routes → report carpet stains or lighting faults to Engineering immediately and follow up to closure.

5夜间开床服务 Evening Turndown Service
中文

敲门问候确认宾客方便后进入 → 翻开被角约 30–45 度，摆放次日日程卡与巧克力/纪念品 → 收拾垃圾、拉好窗帘、调暗灯光 → 将拖鞋与睡衣摆放到位，铺好地巾 → 检查明日靠港天气并留意宾客留言与特殊要求，完成后登记开床记录。

English

Knock, greet and confirm the guest is comfortable with your entry → fold back the top corner of the duvet about 30–45 degrees and place the next day's program card with chocolates or a keepsake → remove trash, close the drapes and dim the lights → set out slippers and pajamas with a fresh bath mat → check tomorrow's port weather and note any guest messages or requests, then record the turndown log.

服务话术Service Scripts

敲门问候 Knock & Greet

客房服务！早上好，先生/女士。请问现在方便打扫房间吗？
Housekeeping! Good morning, sir/madam. May I clean your stateroom now?

毛巾艺术留言 Towel Art Note

这是为您准备的小惊喜——希望这只小天鹅能让您今晚睡个好觉。祝您航程愉快！
Here's a little surprise for you — we hope this swan brings a smile to your face. Sleep well and enjoy the rest of your cruise!

询问清洁时间偏好 Preferred Cleaning Time

请问您希望我在什么时间段打扫房间？上午还是下午？我会按照您方便的时间来安排。
What time would you prefer me to service your stateroom — morning or afternoon? I'll arrange it around your schedule.

宾客请求加物 Guest Request

好的，先生。我马上为您送去两条新毛巾和一个转换插头，大约十分钟后送到。还有什么可以帮您的吗？
Certainly, sir. I'll bring you two fresh towels and an adapter right away — they'll be there in about ten minutes. Is there anything else I can help you with?

道别 Farewell

房间已经打扫好了，毛巾也已更换。祝您今天在船上玩得开心，有任何需要随时拨打客房服务电话。
Your stateroom is ready and the towels have been changed. Enjoy your day on board, and please dial Housekeeping anytime you need anything.

高频面试问答Top Interview Questions

请用英文做一个简单的自我介绍。Self-introduction (English)
参考要点

姓名+教育/工作背景+客房或清洁相关经验+应聘原因+个人特质（吃苦耐劳、细心）。控制在 1 分钟内。

Sample Answer

Good morning, my name is Wang Lei. I worked as a room attendant at a five-star hotel for two years, where I cleaned 14 to 16 rooms a day, handled turndown service, and followed strict hygiene standards. I'm applying for this position because I enjoy hands-on work and take pride in making guests feel at home. I'm hardworking, detail-oriented, and I can adapt well to life on board.

每天要打扫 12–16 间客房，工作强度很大，你能坚持吗？Can you handle cleaning 12–16 staterooms a day?
参考要点

展示体力与心理双重准备：有酒店/清洁工作经验、了解真实工作节奏、给出保持体能的具体方法。避免空喊口号。

Sample Answer

Yes, I understand the workload is heavy — 12 to 16 staterooms a day is a real challenge. From my hotel experience I know how to work efficiently and follow a fixed routine for each cabin. To keep up my energy, I will sleep well, eat properly, and exercise regularly in the crew gym. I'm physically fit and mentally prepared for this pace.

如果宾客投诉房间没有打扫干净，你会怎么处理？A guest complains the cabin was not cleaned properly.
参考要点

先致歉不辩解 → 立即重新清洁或补做 → 请宾客验收 → 上报主管并记录，避免重复发生。展示责任心与补救意识。

Sample Answer

I would apologize first and never argue with the guest. I would re-clean the area or fix the missed items immediately, then ask the guest to confirm everything is satisfactory. Afterwards, I would inform my supervisor and note it down so the same mistake doesn't happen again. Guest satisfaction always comes first.

清洁时发现宾客遗落的贵重物品（如现金、首饰），你会怎么做？You find valuables left in a cabin. What do you do?
参考要点

不私自动、不私自保管 → 立即上报主管 → 按失物招领流程登记上交 → 强调诚实是客房岗位的生命线。

Sample Answer

I would never touch or keep it. I would report to my supervisor immediately and hand it in following the lost and found procedure, with the item recorded and signed for. Honesty is the most important quality for a stateroom attendant — guests trust us with their private space, and integrity is non-negotiable.

宾客在房间挂了「请勿打扰」，但到了开床时间还没取牌，怎么办？A guest hangs a DND sign past turndown time.
参考要点

尊重宾客意愿是第一位 → 不敲门不打扰 → 按流程上报主管 → 在门外留言卡或通过前台询问宾客是否需要服务。

Sample Answer

I would respect the guest's privacy and not knock or enter the cabin. I would report it to my supervisor and follow the standard procedure — usually leaving a card outside or asking Guest Services to contact the guest to see if they still need turndown service later. The guest's comfort and privacy always come first.

你的英语基础一般，如何与来自各国的宾客沟通？How do you communicate with guests if your English is basic?
参考要点

坦诚但展示积极态度：掌握岗位核心服务用语 → 借助肢体语言与纸笔 → 坚持每天学习 → 复杂问题及时请主管协助。

Sample Answer

My English is at a basic conversational level, but I have already memorized the key service expressions for housekeeping, such as greetings, turndown and guest requests. I use body language and a notepad when needed, and I ask my supervisor for help with complicated questions. I also study English every day on board, so I'm confident I will improve quickly through daily practice.
