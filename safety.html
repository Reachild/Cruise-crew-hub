岗前安全与证件指南STCW & Pre-Boarding Guide

上船前的必修课：办齐四大船员证书与其他常见证件，掌握 STCW 基本安全知识，熟悉登船全流程。证件不齐无法上船，安全不过关无法通过 Sign On。

证件办理 STCW 基本安全 登船全流程

四大船员证书Four Essential Seafarer Certificates

证书说明办理机构与周期
海员证Seaman's Book / Seafarer's Passport 船员的身份证件，国际通行的「船员护照」，用于出入境与登船核验。须先持有效护照方可办理。 海事局或授权海事机构办理，约 2–4 周（以当地为准）
船员服务簿Seafarer's Record Book 记录海上服务资历的官方文件，是职级晋升与再签约的凭据。 海事局或海事学校办理，约 1–3 周
STCW 基本安全培训四小证BST Basic Safety Training 含 4 个科目：个人求生、防火与灭火、初级急救、个人安全与社会责任。证书有效期 5 年，到期需复训更新。 海事学校（须为海事局认可的培训机构），全程约 2–4 周
健康证书Medical Certificate 按船旗国标准进行体检，常见 MLC/ILO 标准。检查视力、听力、心肺、传染病等，部分岗位有额外要求。 海事局认可的体检机构，约 1–2 周出证

其他常见证件Other Common Documents

证件说明
护照Passport建议至少覆盖整个合同期并余 6 个月（通常需 12–18 个月），确保覆盖整个合同期及续约需要。
签证Visa按航线要求办理，如美国 C1/D 过境船员签证（美线航线必需）等，以船司入职指引为准。
无犯罪记录证明Police Clearance Certificate (PCC，船员版)户籍地或居住地公安机关出具，部分船司接受船员专用版本，通常须在近 6 个月内开具。
黄热病疫苗证书Yellow Fever Vaccination Certificate前往或经停黄热病流行区的航线要求，国际旅行卫生中心可接种并出具「小黄本」。
英语等级证明English Proficiency Test部分船司要求 Marlins 或 TALA 等船员英语测试成绩，面试通过后安排。
岗位技能证书Position-specific Certificates如厨师健康证（Ship's Cook Certificate）、荷官证（Casino Gaming License）等，视岗位而定。

STCW 基本安全四科目详解BST: Four Modules Explained

1个人求生技能 Personal Survival Techniques
中文

学习弃船时的求生技能：正确穿着救生衣、从高处跳水姿势（一手护鼻、一手压救生衣）、在水中保暖结组、扶正倾覆的救生筏、登筏与筏上求生。实操在泳池完成，须通过跳水与扶筏考核。

English

Covers survival skills for abandoning ship: proper donning of a lifejacket, correct jumping technique (one hand pinching the nose, the other holding down the lifejacket), huddle positions for warmth in water, righting an inverted liferaft, and boarding and surviving on the raft. Practical sessions take place in a swimming pool and include assessed jumps and raft drills.

2基本防火与灭火 Fire Prevention & Fire Fighting
中文

学习「火灾三角」（可燃物、助燃物、点火源）与火灾分类，掌握各类灭火器的选择与实操（水基、泡沫、干粉、二氧化碳），并穿戴自给式呼吸器（SCBA）进入烟雾舱室搜救与灭火演练。

English

Covers the fire triangle (fuel, oxygen, heat) and classes of fire, correct selection and hands-on use of extinguishers (water, foam, dry powder, CO2), and search-and-rescue and firefighting drills in a smoke-filled facility while wearing self-contained breathing apparatus (SCBA).

3初级急救 Elementary First Aid
中文

学习心肺复苏（CPR）与 AED 使用、止血包扎、烧烫伤处理、骨折固定与伤员搬运。重点是「先呼救、再施救」，在专业救援到达前稳定伤情。

English

Covers CPR and AED use, bleeding control and bandaging, burn treatment, fracture immobilization, and casualty transport. The priority is to call for help first, then stabilize the casualty until professional assistance arrives.

4个人安全与社会责任 Personal Safety & Social Responsibility
中文

学习安全作业规范（劳动防护、危险区域识别）、疲劳管理与值班休息制度、防治性骚扰与 bullying、机房与封闭空间作业安全、海洋环保意识（MARPOL 基本要求）。

English

Covers safe working practices (PPE, hazard awareness), fatigue management and rest-hour rules, prevention of harassment and bullying, safe entry into enclosed spaces and machinery areas, and marine environmental awareness under MARPOL.

上船前必背安全知识Must-Know Safety Knowledge Before Joining

#知识要点说明
1应急集合站Muster Station登船后第一件事：找到分配给你的集合站。个人救生衣上挂牌标注的集合站编号必须背记，听到弃船信号立即前往。
2弃船信号Abandon Ship Signal七短一长声（7 short + 1 prolonged），鸣笛或警铃连续播报。听到后穿救生衣、带保暖衣物，立即赴集合站。
3火警信号Fire Alarm Signal连续警铃加广播三遍「Fire, fire, fire」，随后播报起火位置（如「Fire on Deck 5, forward」）。确认位置后按指令行动，不盲目前往。
4救生衣穿着Lifejacket Donning掌握救生衣穿戴方法（donning）：卡扣紧固并收紧束带，检查哨子与示位灯完好，扣紧后不松脱。救生衣在客舱衣柜与工作区各有一件。
5逃生路线Escape Route沿黑底绿人指示标识（绿色疏散标志）走向登艇甲板，绝不乘电梯。记住至少两条备选路线。
6全装应急演练Full Muster Drill按 SOLAS 公约要求，所有船员每周参加完整应急演练。登船 24 小时内须完成旅客弃船演练前的熟悉培训。

上船流程 8 步From Offer to On Board: 8 Steps

#中文English
1签约（通过面试后签署雇佣合同）Sign the employment contract after passing the interview
2办证（海员证、服务簿、STCW 四小证等）Obtain documents: Seaman's Book, Record Book, BST certificates, etc.
3体检（按船旗国/MLC 标准取得健康证书）Pass the medical examination and obtain the Medical Certificate
4等船期（等待船司分配上船时间）Waitlist / Assignment: await your joining date
5机票与集合（收到乘船通知，含航班与集合地点）Receive Joining Instructions: flights, hotel and meeting point
6登船报到（在港口登船并办理入职手续）Sign On at the port and complete onboarding
7安全入职培训（24 小时内熟悉集合站与逃生路线）Safety Induction: know your muster station and escape routes within 24 hours
8试用期（通常 30–60 天，以船司为准）Probation period, typically 30–60 days

高频问答Frequently Asked Questions

办证总费用大概多少？How much do the certificates cost in total?
中文

护照约 120–200 元，海员证、服务簿、体检各约 100–500 元，STCW 四小证培训约 2000–5000 元（各省海事学校价格差异较大）。全部自办合计通常在 3000–8000 元区间，不含签证与机票。以各机构最新收费为准。

English

A passport costs roughly CNY 120–200; the Seaman's Book, Record Book and medical exam each run about CNY 100–500; BST training ranges from CNY 2,000–5,000 depending on the maritime academy. Doing everything yourself typically totals CNY 3,000–8,000, excluding visas and flights. Always check current fees with each authority.

从签约到上船全程要多久？How long does the whole process take?
中文

顺利情况下约 1–3 个月：办证与培训 2–6 周（可并行安排）、体检 1–2 周、之后等船期，从几天到数月不等，取决于船司岗位空缺与航线。热门岗位候补可能更久，建议尽早办证。

English

Expect 1–3 months in a smooth case: documents and training take 2–6 weeks (can run in parallel), the medical exam 1–2 weeks, then waiting for an assignment — from days to months depending on vacancies and itineraries. Start your certificates early to shorten the wait.

没经验可以先上什么岗位？What entry-level jobs can I start with no experience?
中文

无经验常见入口：餐厅助理（Assistant Waiter）、客舱服务助理（Cabin Steward）、洗衣房与厨房杂工（Galley Utility）、 酒吧服务生（Bar Waiter）等基础岗位。英语好可尝试儿童俱乐部助理、摄影助理。先积累 1–2 个合同再申请晋升岗位。

English

Common entry points for first-timers: Assistant Waiter, Cabin Steward, Galley Utility, Laundry and Bar Waiter. With strong English, you may try Youth Staff Assistant or Assistant Photographer. Build 1–2 contracts first, then apply for promoted positions.

中介说要交「培训费打包价」，可信吗？Is an agency's "all-in-one training package fee" trustworthy?
中文

正规流程中，STCW 四小证、海员证等完全可以自行到海事学校和海事局办理，费用透明。警惕动辄上万元的「打包价」：正规中介只收取合理服务费，办证费用应凭票据实报。任何「交钱保证上船」的承诺都涉嫌违规。请对照招聘渠道页的防骗自查表逐项核对。

English

In the standard process, you can obtain the BST certificates and Seaman's Book yourself through maritime academies and authorities at transparent prices. Be cautious of "package fees" running into tens of thousands: legitimate agencies charge a reasonable service fee only, with document costs reimbursed against receipts. Any promise of "guaranteed placement for payment" is a red flag. Cross-check with the anti-fraud checklist on the Recruitment Channels page.
