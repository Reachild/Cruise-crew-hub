前厅接待Receptionist / Guest Services Officer

隶属前台/宾客服务部（Hotel Guest Services），是宾客登船后接触最多、解决问题的关键岗位，被誉为「全船服务的中枢」。

前台/客服部 英语要求：高 起薪与晋升空间优

岗位概览Position Overview

中文

前厅接待负责宾客登船入住、房间变更、账单结算、问询指引与客诉处理，同时承担紧急情况下的宾客疏散引导。该岗位要求英语流利、抗压能力强、具备酒店前台或客服经验。多数船司要求本科或大专学历，部分接受优秀中专生。合同期通常为 6–8 个月，休息日不固定，需两班倒。

English

The Receptionist is responsible for guest embarkation and check-in, stateroom changes, folio settlement, guest inquiries and complaint resolution, as well as guest muster guidance during emergencies. This role requires fluent English, strong resilience under pressure, and front desk or customer service experience. Most cruise lines prefer a college diploma or above, while some accept outstanding vocational-school graduates. Contracts typically run 6–8 months, with rotating shifts and irregular days off.

日常工作流程 SOPStandard Operating Procedure

1登船入住办理 Embarkation Check-in
中文

提前核对当日到港宾客名单 → 分组验票、扫描护照、核对签证与海员信息 → 发放船卡并指引房间位置 → 对老年、轮椅及家庭宾客提供优先通道。全程使用欢迎话术，10 分钟内完成单人办理。

English

Review the arrival guest list in advance → verify tickets, scan passports, and check visas and seafarer information → issue cruise cards and direct guests to staterooms → offer priority lanes for seniors, wheelchair users, and families. Use the standard welcome script and complete each check-in within 10 minutes.

2宾客问询服务 Guest Inquiries
中文

遵循「倾听—确认—回应—跟进」四步法。能当场解答的立即答复；不确定的先记录，查询后 15 分钟内回复宾客；涉及政策类问题须引用船司最新指引，不得凭经验承诺。

English

Follow the four-step model: Listen – Confirm – Respond – Follow up. Answer immediately when possible; for uncertain questions, record them and reply within 15 minutes. Policy questions must reference the cruise line's latest guidelines — never promise based on personal experience.

3客诉处理与补救 Complaint Handling & Service Recovery
中文

使用 LEARN 模型：倾听（Listen）→ 共情（Empathize）→ 道歉（Apologize）→ 解决（Resolve）→ 跟进（Notify）。权限内当场解决；超出权限（如退款超过限额）立即上报当班经理。处理完毕后 24 小时内回访宾客满意度。

English

Apply the LEARN model: Listen, Empathize, Apologize, Resolve, Notify. Resolve within your authority; escalate immediately when beyond limits (e.g., refunds above the threshold). Follow up on guest satisfaction within 24 hours after resolution.

4账务结算 Folio Settlement
中文

离船前一日开放快速结算通道，逐一核对船载账单 → 对争议消费调取签购单 → 处理退款须双人复核 → 最后一天关闭账户并打印结清凭证。所有金额差错须当日上报，不得私自抹账。

English

Open express settlement one day before disembarkation: verify each folio, retrieve receipts for disputed charges, process refunds with double verification, and close accounts with printed confirmation on the final day. Report any discrepancy the same day — never adjust accounts without authorization.

5交接班 Shift Handover
中文

交接内容包括：未结案客诉、待跟进事项、特殊宾客（VIP、纪念日、行动不便）、现金与备用金核对。双方签字确认，重要事项当面口头复述一遍。

English

Hand over: open complaints, pending follow-ups, special guests (VIPs, anniversaries, mobility needs), and cash float reconciliation. Both parties sign off, and critical items are verbally confirmed face to face.

服务话术Service Scripts

欢迎登船 Welcome

欢迎登上「梦想号」！我是前厅接待 Emily，很高兴为您服务。您的房间在 9 层甲板，电梯在您右手边。
Welcome aboard the Dream! I'm Emily from Guest Services. Your stateroom is on Deck 9 — the elevator is on your right.

指引问询 Directions

自助餐厅在 11 层船尾方向，步行大约两分钟。您也可以乘坐中庭的观光电梯，顺便欣赏一下中庭美景。
The buffet restaurant is located aft on Deck 11, about a two-minute walk. You may also take the panoramic elevator in the atrium and enjoy the view on your way.

客诉安抚 Complaint Handling

非常抱歉给您带来了不便，我完全理解您的心情。请允许我立即为您核实，我们一定给您一个满意的解决方案。
I sincerely apologize for the inconvenience. I completely understand how you feel. Please allow me to check on this right away — we will make sure this is resolved to your satisfaction.

账单解释 Folio Clarification

这是您本次航程的消费明细。这两笔是酒吧的消费，如果您认为有误，我可以调取签购单为您核对。
Here is the itemized folio for your cruise. These two charges are from the bar. If you believe there is an error, I can retrieve the signed receipts for verification.

离船送别 Farewell

感谢您选择我们的航线，希望这次旅程给您留下了美好的回忆。期待再次为您服务，祝您一路平安！
Thank you for sailing with us. We hope this voyage has given you wonderful memories. We look forward to welcoming you again — have a safe journey home!

高频面试问答Top Interview Questions

请用英文做一个简单的自我介绍。Self-introduction (English)
参考要点

姓名+教育背景+相关经验（酒店/客服）+应聘原因+个人特质（抗压、亲和）。控制在 1 分钟内。

Sample Answer

Good morning, my name is Li Hua. I graduated from XX College with a diploma in Hospitality Management. I have worked as a front desk agent at a four-star hotel for two years, where I handled check-in, guest complaints, and shift reports. I'm applying for this position because I'm passionate about international hospitality, and I thrive in fast-paced, multicultural environments. I'm patient, detail-oriented, and I always put the guest first.

如果宾客对房间噪音投诉，你会怎么处理？How would you handle a noise complaint?
参考要点

先致歉并共情 → 立即核实噪音来源 → 提供解决方案（调房/耳塞/工程处理）→ 跟进回访。展示 LEARN 模型。

Sample Answer

First, I would apologize sincerely and empathize with the guest. Then I would contact the relevant department to identify the noise source immediately. Depending on the situation, I would offer solutions such as a stateroom change, earplugs, or an engineering fix. Finally, I would follow up with the guest to confirm the issue has been fully resolved.

宾客要求你承诺退款，但超出你的权限怎么办？A guest demands a refund beyond your authority.
参考要点

不私自承诺，也不生硬拒绝。安抚宾客 → 记录诉求 → 立即上报当班经理 → 明确告知宾客处理时限。

Sample Answer

I would never make a promise beyond my authority. I would calm the guest, take detailed notes of the request, and escalate it to the duty manager immediately. Then I would inform the guest of the expected response time so they know their concern is being taken seriously.

邮轮工作要离开家人 6–8 个月，你如何应对？How do you cope with being away from family for 6–8 months?
参考要点

展示心理准备与成熟度：与家人已充分沟通、可定期视频、把船期视为职业成长机会。避免表现出犹豫。

Sample Answer

My family fully supports my career choice. We have discussed it carefully, and we will keep in touch through video calls during my contract. I see a shipboard contract as a valuable opportunity to grow professionally and financially, and I am mentally prepared for the rhythm of life on board.

如果同事在繁忙时段与你发生争执，你会怎么处理？How do you handle a conflict with a colleague during a busy shift?
参考要点

以宾客服务优先 → 保持冷静不扩大矛盾 → 事后私下沟通或找主管协调 → 强调团队协作。

Sample Answer

Guest service always comes first. During the shift I would stay calm and focus on my tasks instead of escalating the conflict. After the shift, I would talk to my colleague privately to clear up the misunderstanding, or involve my supervisor if needed. Team harmony is essential, especially in a multinational crew.

你能接受两班倒和连续工作吗？如何保持状态？Can you handle rotating shifts and long working hours?
参考要点

给出具体方法：规律作息、船上健身房锻炼、与室友协调、利用港口休息日恢复。展示你了解船上的真实工作强度。

Sample Answer

Yes, I understand shipboard life means rotating shifts and long hours. I will keep a regular sleep schedule, exercise in the crew gym, and rest properly during port days. I also plan to manage my energy by staying organized during peak hours, so I can maintain consistent service quality throughout my contract.
