餐厅服务生Waiter / Waitress

隶属餐饮部（Food & Beverage），是宾客用餐体验的塑造者，也是邮轮上小费收入最可观的岗位之一，被誉为「餐桌上的形象大使」。

餐饮部 英语要求：中 小费收入可观

岗位概览Position Overview

中文

餐厅服务生负责餐前摆台与备品准备、迎宾点单、上菜与餐中服务、酒水推介以及收台翻台，直接影响宾客对餐饮质量的评价。该岗位要求具备一定的英语听说能力、规范的西餐服务知识与良好的体力和亲和力。新人通常从助理服务生（Assistant Waiter）起步，表现优异可晋升为服务生（Waiter），进而晋升领班（Head Waiter）。合同期通常为 6–8 个月，每天两餐高峰时段工作，靠港日与航行日工作强度相近。

English

The Waiter / Waitress is responsible for side station setup, greeting and order taking, food service, beverage recommendation, and clearing and resetting tables — directly shaping the guests' dining experience. This role requires working English communication skills, knowledge of western table service standards, physical stamina, and a warm personality. New hires usually start as Assistant Waiters and can be promoted to Waiter and then Head Waiter based on performance. Contracts typically run 6–8 months, covering two main dining periods each day, with similar intensity on sea days and port days.

日常工作流程 SOPStandard Operating Procedure

1开餐前准备 Side Station Setup
中文

餐前 30 分钟到岗 → 按标准摆台（餐具、口布、酒杯）→ 补齐备品架（刀叉、口布、面包篮、调料）→ 核对当日菜单与 86 单（售罄菜品）→ 参加班前会，确认预订与特殊宾客信息。责任区须通过领班开餐前检查方可迎客。

English

Report 30 minutes before service → set tables to standard (cutlery, napkins, glassware) → restock the side station (silverware, napkins, bread baskets, condiments) → review the daily menu and the 86 list (sold-out items) → attend the pre-shift briefing on reservations and special guests. Your station must pass the Head Waiter's inspection before greeting guests.

2迎宾点单 Greeting & Order Taking
中文

微笑迎宾并称呼宾客姓氏 → 拉椅入座、展开口布、递上菜单 → 介绍当日推荐与特色菜品 → 采用「先饮品、后头盘、再主菜」顺序点单 → 复述订单确认 → 登录 POS 并向厨房传达过敏与忌口。全程遵循「女士与年长宾客优先」原则。

English

Greet guests with a smile and use their names whenever possible → seat them, unfold napkins, and present menus → introduce daily specials and signature dishes → take orders in sequence: drinks first, then appetizers, then main courses → repeat the order to confirm → enter it into the POS and pass allergy and dietary notes to the galley. Always serve ladies and senior guests first.

3上菜与餐中服务 Food Service
中文

从宾客右侧上菜、撤盘 → 上菜时报菜名，摆放时主菜居中 → 掌握用餐节奏，避免上菜过快或过慢 → 每上一道菜后约 2 分钟主动回台询问满意度 → 发现宾客需要（加水、补酒、换餐具）应在开口前主动提供。

English

Serve and clear plates from the guest's right → announce each dish by name with the main course centered → pace the courses so they are neither rushed nor delayed → check back about 2 minutes after each course is served → anticipate needs (water refills, another glass, fresh cutlery) before guests have to ask.

4酒水推介 Beverage Recommendation
中文

掌握当班酒单与推广酒品 → 依据菜品推荐搭配酒款（如海鲜配白葡萄酒）→ 介绍酒款产区与口感时简洁易懂 → 开瓶后按规范示瓶、试酒、斟酒（红白标准杯量）→ 上甜品时推介咖啡、茶或餐后甜酒。酒水推介直接关联部门营收与小费。

English

Know the wine list and the promotion of the day → suggest pairings based on the dishes (e.g., white wine with seafood) → keep descriptions of regions and flavors short and easy to understand → follow the proper service sequence: present the bottle, pour a tasting sip, then serve (respecting standard pours for red and white) → recommend coffee, tea or dessert wine with dessert. Beverage sales directly drive department revenue and your tips.

5收台与翻台 Clearing & Resetting
中文

征得宾客同意后按「由内到外」撤盘 → 玻璃杯用托盘单独撤运，防止碰碎 → 台面用口布清理碎屑 → 按开餐标准重新摆台 → 污染餐具分拣入洗碗间，尖锐物品单独处理 → 翻台全程不超过 10 分钟，且不影响相邻用餐宾客。

English

Ask permission before clearing and work from the inside out → carry glassware separately on a tray to prevent breakage → brush crumbs with a folded napkin → reset the table to opening standard → sort soiled dishes for the scullery and handle sharp items separately → complete each turnaround within 10 minutes without disturbing neighboring guests.

服务话术Service Scripts

开餐欢迎 Welcome & Seating

晚上好，欢迎光临主餐厅！我是您的服务生 Jason，很荣幸今晚为您服务。请问几位用餐？这边请。
Good evening, welcome to the Main Dining Room! My name is Jason and I'll be taking care of you tonight. How many in your party? This way, please.

当日菜单推介 Daily Specials

今晚的招牌菜是香煎鲈鱼配柠檬黄油汁，是主厨特别推荐。请问您准备好点单了吗？
Tonight's signature dish is the pan-seared sea bass with lemon butter sauce — the chef's special recommendation. Are you ready to order?

酒水推荐 Beverage Recommendation

这道牛排搭配我们的赤霞珠红葡萄酒非常合适，口感醇厚。要不要来一杯？或者我先为您续上饮品？
Our Cabernet Sauvignon pairs beautifully with the steak — it's smooth and full-bodied. Would you like a glass? Or shall I bring you another round of drinks?

宾客满意致谢 Guest Satisfaction

请问今晚的菜品还合您口味吗？很高兴听到您的认可，您的满意就是我们最大的动力。
How was everything tonight? I'm so glad to hear you enjoyed it — your satisfaction means the world to us.

道别 Farewell

感谢您的光临，期待明晚再次为您服务。祝您晚安，做个好梦！
Thank you for dining with us. We look forward to serving you again tomorrow evening. Good night and sleep well!

高频面试问答Top Interview Questions

请用英文做一个简单的自我介绍。Self-introduction (English)
参考要点

姓名+教育/培训背景+餐饮或服务经验+应聘原因+个人特质（体力好、亲和、抗压）。控制在 1 分钟内。

Sample Answer

Good morning, my name is Wang Lei. I completed my hospitality training at XX College with a focus on food and beverage service. I have worked as a server in a western restaurant for two years, where I did table setting, order taking, and wine recommendation. I'm applying for this position because I love creating memorable dining experiences, and I'm hardworking, energetic, and a real team player.

如果宾客抱怨菜品上得太慢，你会怎么处理？How would you handle a guest complaining about slow food service?
参考要点

先致歉共情 → 立即向厨房/领班核实催菜 → 向宾客说明大致等待时间 → 可提供面包或饮品安抚 → 出菜后跟进确认。展示主动补救意识。

Sample Answer

I would apologize sincerely first and let the guest know I understand their frustration. Then I would check with the galley and the Head Waiter right away to follow up on the order, and give the guest a realistic waiting time. If needed, I would offer bread or a complimentary drink to make the wait comfortable. Once the food is served, I would check back to make sure everything is to their satisfaction.

宾客说菜品不合口味要求退菜，你怎么办？A guest asks to send a dish back because they don't like it.
参考要点

不争辩、不质疑宾客 → 致歉并询问具体原因 → 确认是口味问题还是质量问题 → 及时上报领班按流程处理 → 尽快推荐替代菜品。强调宾客感受优先。

Sample Answer

I would never argue with the guest. I would apologize, ask politely what the issue is, and find out whether it's a matter of taste or quality. Then I would inform my Head Waiter and handle it according to procedure. Most importantly, I would quickly suggest an alternative dish so the guest still enjoys their meal. The guest's experience always comes first.

高峰时段你负责的区域多桌同时用餐，如何安排优先顺序？How do you prioritize multiple tables during peak hours?
参考要点

说明优先级原则：先处理宾客当前需求（退菜/催菜）→ 照顾年长与带小孩的餐桌 → 点单与上菜按各桌进度穿插 → 必要时请助理服务生或同事支援。展示条理性与团队协作。

Sample Answer

I would handle immediate guest needs first, such as complaints or rush orders, and pay special attention to tables with seniors or families. Then I would flexibly coordinate order taking and food service across all my tables, following each table's pace, so no one waits too long. If it gets really busy, I would ask my Assistant Waiter or a colleague for support — teamwork is how we keep service smooth during peak hours.

如何向宾客推介酒水以提高酒水销售额？How do you upsell beverages to increase beverage revenue?
参考要点

强调「推介而非强卖」：熟悉酒单 → 结合菜品做搭配推荐 → 用选择式问句（A 还是 B）→ 简洁介绍口感 → 尊重宾客决定。体现销售意识与服务分寸。

Sample Answer

Good upselling is recommendation, not pressure. I would know the wine list well and suggest pairings that genuinely match the guest's dishes — for example, a crisp white wine with seafood. I like to offer choices, such as "Would you prefer the house red or the reserve selection?", and describe the flavor briefly. If the guest declines, I simply respect their choice and keep providing great service.

邮轮餐饮工作强度大、几乎没有完整休息日，你如何应对？How do you cope with the demanding schedule and few full days off on board?
参考要点

展示心理准备：已了解餐饮部真实节奏 → 保持体能（锻炼、规律作息）→ 利用港前港后碎片时间休息 → 把船期视为收入与成长机会。避免表现出犹豫。

Sample Answer

Yes, I understand that restaurant work on board means long hours during meal periods and very few full days off. I'm physically prepared — I keep fit and maintain a regular sleep schedule, and I'll use short breaks between meal services to recharge. I see a shipboard contract as a great opportunity to earn well, improve my English, and grow from Assistant Waiter to Waiter, so the demanding schedule is worth it for me.
