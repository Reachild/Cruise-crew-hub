跨洋大型邮轮 Ocean Cruise Lines

🚢皇家加勒比国际Royal Caribbean International (RCI)规模 Top 2英语：高设施科技感强▼
中文介绍

全球第二大邮轮公司，总部位于美国迈阿密。以「海洋量子号」「海洋奇迹号」等大型高科技船只闻名，船上设施丰富（冲浪模拟器、攀岩墙、北极星观景舱），年轻宾客比例高，节奏快、标准高。在华通过指定合作代理招聘中国船员，热门岗位竞争激烈。管理规范、培训体系完善，是行业公认的「职业履历加分项」。

About

Headquartered in Miami, RCI is one of the world's largest cruise lines, famous for innovative megaships such as Quantum and Wonder of the Seas with features like surf simulators, rock walls and the North Star. The guest demographic is young and energetic, so the pace is fast and service standards are demanding. RCI is well known for structured training and career development — an RCI contract is a strong resume highlight.

🚢嘉年华邮轮Carnival Cruise Line规模 Top 1英语：高小费成熟▼
中文介绍

全球运力规模最大的邮轮公司，总部在美国，主打「欢乐邮轮（Fun Ship）」概念，航线以加勒比海为主，美国短航线客人多、小费文化成熟。船上工作强度大但氛围轻松，对英语口语要求高（客源几乎全为英语母语者）。招聘渠道以菲律宾马尼拉等基地港为主，中国船员岗位相对有限，适合英语好的求职者。

About

Carnival operates the largest fleet in the world and is known as the "Fun Ships", sailing mainly in the Caribbean with a mostly American, English-speaking guest base and an established tipping culture. Crew hiring runs mainly through Manila (Philippines) and Mumbai (India) crew hubs, so openings for Chinese crew are relatively limited. The workload is intense but the onboard atmosphere is relaxed. Since nearly all guests are native English speakers, strong spoken English is essential.

🚢地中海邮轮MSC Cruises规模 Top 3零经验友好大量招中国船员▼
中文介绍

欧洲最大邮轮公司，总部在瑞士日内瓦，船队以意大利风格设计著称，近年在华部署「MSC 地中海荣耀号」等船只，大量招聘中国船员，是新人最常选择的船司之一。多国客人构成（意、德、法、中），服务场景多元；内部晋升快，但管理风格偏欧式、纪律严格。中国籍员工数量多，新人融入相对容易。

About

MSC is Europe's largest cruise line, headquartered in Geneva with distinctive Italian-style ships. It has deployed ships in China and recruits Chinese crew extensively, making it one of the most accessible lines for beginners. The multinational guest mix (Italian, German, French, Chinese) creates diverse service scenarios. Promotion can be fast, though the management style is European and highly disciplined.

About

NCL pioneered "Freestyle Cruising" — no fixed seating or dining times — which makes F&B roles more flexible. Ships feature large waterparks and Broadway-style shows. It recruits a moderate number of Chinese crew, and interviews focus on spoken English and service flexibility. Contracts typically run 7–8 months.

欧洲内河邮轮 European River Cruise Lines

💡 内河邮轮小知识：欧洲内河船（莱茵河、多瑙河、塞纳河等）吨位小、靠港频繁、单航次 7–14 天。船员多为「一站式多岗」：餐厅员工同时承担客舱整理等工作。工作节奏快、靠近岸生活，但合同期较短（常见 4–6 个月），转正与续约快。对想积累欧洲服务经验、适应多元文化的中国船员是很好的起点。River ships on the Rhine, Danube and Seine are small with frequent ports. Crew often take combined roles (e.g., waiting + cabin service). Contracts are shorter (4–6 months) — a great start for building European service experience.
